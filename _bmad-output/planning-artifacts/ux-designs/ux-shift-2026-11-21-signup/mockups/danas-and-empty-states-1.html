1 · Kartica Postavljanje na Danas (UX-DR69)

Drawn for an active admin of an organization founded by signup, until no fact step is left or it is dismissed (AD-24); an operator-provisioned organization never sees it. It is first on admin Danas, above Treba tebe, because in an organization with no rotation it is the only work there is. It counts the five fact steps in words and numbers ({n} od 5, three plural forms), lists those not done, each a link to its step, and offers Nastavi: {prvi} and Sakrij. It is outside Danas's freshness set (as the loan line is): drawn once its reads are there, never holding the skeleton.

OdustaniSakrij postavljanje

2 · Prazna stanja nove organizacije (UX-DR70)

Every destination an admin can open before setup is finished says what is true and why, in organization-neutral words, and where setup is not done offers the way to it. The reason is named, as for an inactive member: "no team exists" is never shown as "no team works today". Existing strings that already hold are kept (Nijedna smjena nije u upotrebi…, Ništa ne čeka odluku., Za rotaciju je potrebna barem jedna smjena u upotrebi.); the new or changed ones are in UX-DR70.
