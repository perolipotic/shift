> **Peer contract.** This file owns *how it works*; `DESIGN.md` owns *how it looks*. Visual tokens are referenced here by name as `{colors.token}` and are defined there, never duplicated. Both spines win on conflict with any mock, wireframe, or import. Product requirements live in `SPEC.md` and its companions and are referenced, not restated.

## Foundation

**Form factor.** Responsive web application. Mobile, tablet, and desktop; no native application. Members are assumed to be primarily on phones — a volunteer between other tasks, in a vehicle, mid-shift, before bed. Admins are primarily on desktop or tablet, with every administrative task also completable on mobile, degraded in comfort but never in capability.

**UI system.** shadcn/ui on React + TypeScript + Vite + Tailwind. Both spines inherit from it. `DESIGN.md` specifies only the brand-layer visual delta; this file specifies only the behavioural delta. Where shadcn defines a behaviour — focus trapping in Dialog, dismissal in Sheet, filtering in Command — that behaviour is inherited and not respecified here.

**Themes.** Light and dark both ship. The default follows `prefers-color-scheme`; a three-option control (Sustav / Svijetla / Tamna) pins either theme per device (human decision 2026-09-25). It lives beside Odjava: in the *Više* sheet on a phone and in the profile menu on desktop (story 7.3). Consequence for every screen: state must be legible in both themes, and no state may rely on one theme's contrast.

**Localization.** Croatian is the only complete locale. Every string resolves through a translation key; none is hard-coded. See the spec's `localization.md` for the full contract — it is binding here, particularly the three-form plural rule, which affects every count this document specifies.

**Motion.** Minimal. Component transitions inherited from shadcn; the product adds none. No motion tokens exist.

## Information Architecture

**Navigation shape.** Bottom tabs on mobile, sidebar on desktop. Two layouts, one architecture.

**Phone bar (< 640 px) — four tabs and *Više*** (story 7.3). Five equal cells; the bar never scrolls sideways, so the current tab is always on screen. The four tabs are fixed per role: a member's are Danas, Kalendar, Sati, Godišnji; an admin's are Danas, Kalendar, Za odluku, Ljudi (*Za odluku* took *Raspored*'s slot in story 9.1). *Više* opens a modal bottom sheet (focus trapped, Escape and a backdrop press close it, focus returns to *Više*) that shows who is signed in (name, role · organization) and then, in binding order: *Pregled* — the role's other places of daily work (Sati, Godišnji for an admin — the leave overview, story 7.15; Ljudi for a member, story 7.17); *Postavke* — the configuration (Postavke rotacije, Organizacija); *Prikaz* — the theme; *Obavijesti* (story 10.3), for every role; *Instaliraj* (story 8.4), only where it can do something; and Odjava. **The member roster is one page for a member (story 7.17).** *Ljudi* is role-scoped: for a member-role session it is the directory — every active team as a group with today's members, each line `Ime · čin · položaj` (rank and position only where the organization uses them), the member's own team first and marked *tvoja smjena*, the rest by name, each group with its count (`0 osoba` included), and *Traži osobu*, a name-only search in `?trazi=` that hides teams with no hit and offers *Poništi pretragu* when nothing matches. It is read-only: no line links anywhere, no write is offered, and it never shows an allowance, balance, leave, hours, username, address or level (CAP-5, FR-16). A member reaches it from *Više* → *Pregled* on a phone and from the sidebar on a desktop. One team's roster also stays in Team detail, reached from Danas.

## Voice and Tone

Microcopy rules. Brand voice lives in `DESIGN.md` § Brand & Style.

- **State the fact, never the absence.** `Danas ne radiš` — never an empty area the member must interpret. Every empty state says what is true, not that nothing was found.
- **Numbers, not adjectives.** A warning states its consequence numerically: `24 h bez pauze`, `2 od 3 člana`. Never "significant", never "warning: check this".
- **No exclamation marks. No encouragement. No personality where a fact will do.** The member is not being congratulated for working nights.
- **Second person singular, informal** (`ne radiš`, not `ne radite`). A volunteer fire brigade is not a corporation. Consistent everywhere.
- **Times are ranges, dates are Croatian-formatted.** `19:00–07:00`, `12.09.2026`. En dash for ranges, never a hyphen.
- **Counts respect three plural forms** — one / few / other. `1 dan`, `2 dana`, `5 dana`; `1 čeka odluku`, `2 čekaju odluku`, `5 čeka odluku`. A `count === 1` check is a defect.
- **Never say "shift" for a team.** `Smjena` is the team; `Tip smjene` is the shift type. Because `Sve smjene` reads ambiguously as an all-teams filter, that control shows a count — `Sve smjene (4)` — and groups its options under a labelled heading.
- **Rank and position** (sprint change 2026-09-25). The member's rank is `Vatrogasni čin`; the team role is `Položaj`, never *pozicija*; the setting is `Vatrogasni činovi i položaji`. The roster line is `Ime · čin · položaj`, labels lowercase beside the name. Neither is ever colour-coded, and neither is a filter in MVP.

## Component Patterns

*Rendered references: [mobile-calendar-1.html](mockups/mobile-calendar-1.html) (shift-cell, mode switch, duty-block) · [conflict-resolution-1.html](mockups/conflict-resolution-1.html) (resolution-option, consequence-strip, legend).*

Behavioural specs. Visual specs are in `DESIGN.md` § Components.

- **shift-cell.** Label always visible; time range appears when width allows and is dropped, never abbreviated, when it does not. Tapping opens Day detail. Modifiers compose — a cell may be both overridden and in conflict, and shows both glyphs.
- **Calendar mode switch.** A segmented control, two modes: *Moj raspored* (default on mobile for a Member Role) and *Sve smjene*. Mode persists across month navigation within a session.
- **Month toolbar (story 7.4).** *Kalendar* and *Sati* share one control, ‹ month ▾ ›, a group named "Mjesec". The month's name is a button that opens the month picker, a non-modal popover named "Odaberi mjesec". The picker shows the year with ‹ › for the year (disabled at the calendar's first and last year) and twelve months by short name, four to a row. The shown month is filled and marked current; today's month has an inset ring and "ovaj" under its name. Each month button is named in full, "Ožujak 2025" (", ovaj mjesec" on today's month), and the year is announced as it changes. The picker opens on the shown month's year, with focus on the shown month. ←/→ move by a month and ↑/↓ by a row, crossing into the year before or after; Enter or a press picks. Picking the shown month only closes it. Escape and a press outside close it and return focus to the month button. Tabbing out of it closes it and leaves focus where it went, and any change of the month (PgUp/PgDn, browser Back) closes it. Where ‹, › or the year's ‹ › reach a bound and disable under focus, focus moves to the month button, or within the picker to its month, never to the page. Since story 15.7b the control is only ‹ month ›: no label and no `Ovaj mjesec` button beside it on any month. On the current month the month button's name adds ", ovaj mjesec"; the way back from any other month is the picker footer's `Ovaj mjesec`, which drops the month from the URL, keeps every other parameter and returns focus to the month button. From 640 px the control sits in the page header, at the right of the title; below 640 px it stays at the top of the screen's card, in the sticky head. The month's heading stays for assistive technology, visually hidden: it names the grid and the day list and takes focus when a closed day detail's opener is gone. At 320 px ‹ month › fits without sideways scroll. The picker's footer names its keys, "← → mjesec · ↑ ↓ red" (from 640 px, where there are keys to press), and holds `Ovaj mjesec`, which picks the current month. Below 640 px the picker is not the popover but the docked (bottom) Dialog sheet, modal, titled "Odaberi mjesec" with its close, opening on the shown month with focus on it; crossing 640 px closes either.
- **Filter chips (stories 7.5, 15.7).** *Kalendar*'s *Sve smjene* and *Sati*'s organization table share one filter bar at the top of the screen's card, built from live records and never hard-coded options, and since story 15.7 it is one pattern at every width, shared with *Ljudi* and *Godišnji*'s overview. A summary card holds the summary line (`role="status"`, always shown) that says what is shown — "Prikazano: sve smjene (4) · 17 osoba", "Prikazano: Smjena B · 4 osobe od 17", "Prikazano: Luka Knežević · Smjena A", or on *Sati* "Prikazano: Smjena B i Luka Knežević · 0 osoba" — and `Filtri`, a button that says it opens a dialog, with a Badge counting the filters on (named "Filtri, aktivno: 2"; just `Filtri` with none). Under the card each active filter is a removable chip, `Smjena: Smjena B` or `Osoba: Luka Knežević`, whose body opens the sheet at that filter's field and whose 44 px ✕ is named "Ukloni filtar smjena: Smjena B" or "Ukloni filtar osoba: Luka Knežević"; after it, focus goes to the next chip, or else to `Filtri`. While a filter is on, `Očisti sve` follows the chips: it clears both, keeps the screen and moves focus to `Filtri`. Focus after a ✕, `Očisti sve` or the empty *Sati* table's ways out moves once the change has rendered, to `Filtri` where the chip it names is not drawn (the empty table's to the first chip left). `Filtri` opens the sheet, at the bottom below 1024 px and at the right from it, with focus on its first field (on the pressed chip's field when a chip opened it). It holds a Smjena field and an Osoba field — each its label and the value chosen — with "U Kalendaru osoba zamjenjuje smjenu." in *Kalendar*, where, while a person replaces the team, the Smjena field reads "Zamijenjeno osobom" and its picker marks no team. Only the control that opened the sheet says it is expanded. A field opens its picker, a non-modal popover named "Odaberi smjenu" or "Odaberi osobu", with an empty search and focus on the chosen option. The search narrows the list as it is typed, with diacritics ignored (Osoba's "Traži osobu (17)" with "1 od 17" beneath it), and says "Nema rezultata" when nothing else matches; ↓ from it enters the list and a character typed on the list goes to it. The Smjena picker is one choice among `Sve smjene (4)` and each team, every team with its person count ("4 osobe"), the teams under a "Smjene" heading; the Osoba picker lists `Sve osobe (17)` and the people, grouped by their team for the month, with `Bez smjene` last. Options are buttons in one tab stop that ↑ ↓ Home End move, the chosen one pressed. Enter or a press picks, writes the URL at once (so Back works) and returns focus to the field; Escape closes only the picker, focus to its field; tabbing out closes it where focus went. A person's team for the month is their membership on their last active date of the month, one rule for both screens. In *Kalendar* a person replaces the team: choosing one drops the team, and its chip goes. On *Sati* the two combine. The sheet's footer has `Poništi` and a live `Prikaži {n} osoba`. The sheet closes on `Prikaži…`, Escape or its close, and when the viewport crosses 1024 px, focus to `Filtri`; every opening starts with an empty search. All filter state lives in the URL (`?smjena=…&osoba=…`), kept across month changes. *Moj raspored* shows no filters; in their place one sentence says whose schedule it is and that the filters belong to *Sve smjene*: "Tvoj raspored · Smjena A. Filtri vrijede za „Sve smjene”.", or "Tvoj raspored · bez smjene. …" on no team for the month. Below 640 px the month toolbar (on *Kalendar* with the mode switch) is a sticky head held at the top of the viewport while the month scrolls, on *Kalendar* and on *Sati*, at most about 160 px; the filter bar follows in flow, and once it has scrolled under the head, the head shows the active filters as one line (visual only: the bar's own summary is the status line). The grid's own scroller fits below the head, so the team header is never under it. *Sati*'s old `?tim=` is replaced by `?smjena=` with every other parameter kept, while `sort=tim` stays a sort value. While the month loads, the toolbar shows and the bar waits for it.
- **Ljudi's filters (story 7.13).** The member list opens on the members active today. Beside the search (`Pretraga po imenu ili adresi`, by name or address with diacritics ignored) sit three option chips — `Razina: sve|Administrator|Član`, `Smjena: sve|<team>|Bez smjene` and `Status: aktivni|neaktivni|svi` — drawn and driven as the 7.5 chips are since story 15.7: the summary card with `Filtri` and its count, only the set chips, each with its ✕ (focus to the next set chip after it, else `Filtri`), and a sheet with one field per chip (Razina, Smjena, Status), whose searchable picker lists each option with its person count over the other filters. `Status` is set by default: `Status: aktivni` is a drawn chip that counts in the Badge, and its ✕ shows `svi`. One summary line (`role="status"`) counts the rows shown: "Prikazano: 15 osoba · 1 administrator · 1 zakazana promjena", every count stated at zero; while today is unknown it states no scheduled count. `Očisti sve` shows only while the list differs from its defaults, and returns them all — no search, every level, every team, `aktivni`, by name ascending — moving focus to the search. The search, filters and sort live in the URL (`?trazi=&razina=&smjena=&status=&sort=`, each absent at its default; `sort=-ime` is descending), so a filtered list can be linked, reloaded and restored by Back. Every chip or sort change is a history entry, and typing replaces the entry. An unknown value falls back to its default, and a team nobody is on any more to every team. The Status column states one fact per row from the member page's own rule: `Aktivno`, `Neaktivno`, or a change dated after today as `Od {dd.mm.}: neaktivno|aktivno` — a scheduled reactivation included. The name carries no status. While the list loads, the chips show the URL's state and the rows are skeletons.
- **Add a member (story 7.13b).** *Dodaj osobu* in Ljudi's header is a button that opens one dialog, *Nova osoba*, over the list; `/ljudi?dodaj=1` is its open state, beside the filters. Opening pushes a history entry, so Back closes it; closing replaces the entry; every filter change keeps it open. The lines are notes, never `destructive`: nothing is disabled, gated or confirmed by them. In the amend form they sit inside the preview's polite live region. After the write lands, its status line repeats the lines captured just before it. If the resolutions cannot be read, one line says "Ne mogu provjeriti zamjene za ove datume." and the save still goes. The member's own *Godišnji* is untouched.
- **Replacement candidates (story 5.4c).** Choosing "Zamijeni osobu" opens "Tko odrađuje smjenu" after the cards — a sibling, because each card is a button and the cards are one arrow-key group — as its own arrow-key radio group with nobody picked. The candidates are the members active that date, less everyone on that team's roster and anyone already put on it, in three groups, each by name: `slobodan`, `radi taj dan` (a working shift on any team that date, or one on an adjacent date that touches the shift), and `odsutnost taj dan` (which wins over the other two). Each line reads `Ime · čin · položaj`, as the roster form's, and wraps rather than truncates. As built (D717, 2026-10-13): where putting the member on would join the shift to their own touching shifts into one duty (story 6.2's derivation, adjacent dates included), the line ends in the real figure, `· 24 h bez pauze`; a shift that would be all there is, or overlaps one of theirs, adds nothing — never a blanket `24 h`. The groups only inform: every candidate is selectable, none is disabled or recommended. With the card chosen and nobody picked, `Spremi odluku` stays disabled and the line says "Odaberi tko odrađuje smjenu."; pressing it moves focus to the first candidate. With nobody to pick at all, the line says "Nitko ne može preuzeti ovu smjenu. Odaberi drugu odluku." and pressing it returns focus to the card. Moving to another card drops the pick, so coming back starts with nobody. A candidate put on the shift meanwhile is refused by name ("… je već na ovoj smjeni. Odaberi nekoga drugoga."), the list is read again without them, and focus moves to it. The reason is clipped to 0026's 200 characters. The override is written with the reason "Zamjena za {ime} (odsutnost)"; there is no reason field.
- **A replacement that no longer applies (story 5.5d).** A replacement counts only while its roster change applies. Once that change is removed in the calendar, left pending by a later rotation save, or inert (the replacement inactive that day, or already on the roster), the shift is really uncovered: the conflict is back in the queue, its ring is back on the calendar, and *Sati* counts the absent member's shift as work and as a conflict again, for the admin and for the member alike. Removing the change in the calendar ends the decision too, attributed to whoever removed it, and asks nothing. A pending change confirmed in the builder's *Izmjene za pregled* (0039) applies again, and the decision with it; amending who arrives keeps the decision, carried by the new change. While a pending or inert change still holds the decision, the resolution screen says "Zamjena se ne primjenjuje. Ukloni je u kalendaru pa odluči ponovno." as the hint beside `Spremi odluku`, which stays disabled; "Ukloni je u kalendaru" links to that month in *Sve smjene*, narrowed to the team, where the day's pending or inert change is removed. A save that still reaches a held decision is refused with the same line. Accepted as they are (human, 2026-10-05): a decision is about the member's absence from that team's shift that day, so it survives a change of the shift type; a decision left behind by a team move hides nothing.
- **Resolution screen navigation.** ‹ › outline icon buttons beside the heading move to the adjacent unresolved conflict in the queue's order, without saving (decision 20, the navigation half). Each is disabled at its end, its label names the target's date, and both show at every width. The heading's line says the place in the queue ("2 od 7"), and the way back above it carries the count.
- **consequence-strip.** Three terms, always the same three, always in this order: coverage, the absent member (labelled *Odsutni član*: their leave day on cards 1–2, their hours as work on card 3), the leave balance. Present on every resolution option so outcomes are compared rather than guessed. Three columns at every width, a phone's included, across the card under a divider. As shipped for "Prihvati kao nepokriveno" (story 5.4b): "3 od 4 člana" with Phosphor `CircleDashedBold` (lucide `CircleDashed` before story 15.1) and "Nepokriveno"; "1 dan odsutnosti" — the conflict's date as a day of absence, whether or not its type draws; leave is counted in days, never hours (human decision 2026-10-10); "16 dana preostalo" over "bez promjene", the balance of the leave year that holds the conflict's date. For "Zamijeni osobu" (story 5.4c): the coverage one higher ("4 od 4 člana"), then "· {ime}" once someone is picked, never "Nepokriveno"; the hours and the balance as the first card's, because the absent member stays rostered on leave. For "Izmijeni odsutnost" (story 5.4d): "4 od 4 člana · {ime} radi"; "12 h rada" (`—` untimed); "17 dana preostalo" over "+1 dan" — the balance once the dropped days are given back, and what that gives back, in the conflict date's leave year. While *Najmanje ljudi u smjeni* is set (story 11.2), each card's coverage reads people at work over its own after against the minimum instead: "rade 2 od najmanje 3" with `UsersThree` and "premalo ljudi" on accepting, "rade 3 od najmanje 3" on replacing or amending; with the setting empty it stays as above.
- **Legend.** Persistent wherever modifier glyphs render. Not a tooltip, not behind an info icon — four glyphs is a vocabulary, and a vocabulary needs to be on screen.
- **Configuration stepper (mobile).** Rotation configuration on a phone is a four-step sequence — shift types, pattern, offsets, preview — with completed steps navigable backwards. On tablet and desktop the same content is one scrolling panel with no stepper. Same data, same validations, same order.
- **Rotation save bar.** As shipped (story 5.5c, mockup `setup-1.html` §2, decision 25): the builder's one "Spremi rotaciju" sits in a bar at the end of the builder (since story 7.18 the history is a dialog behind a header button) — not in the page header. Since story 15.4 the bar is a floating glass pill (`glass-over-content`, `rounded-3xl`) inside the page padding, with `foreground` text; before, it was full width on the card background behind a top border. The bar is a region named "Spremanje rotacije", rendered only once the builder has a draft. While the admin holds unsaved changes (their own draft, even one edited back by hand) the bar shows the hint "Promjena vrijedi od {datum}. · na snazi je rotacija od {datum}." (only the first part while no version is in force; nothing while `Vrijedi od` is empty), in a polite live region, and "Odbaci promjene", a borderless chrome button (story 15.4; an outline before), disabled while a save is in flight. Discarding asks nothing, because it drops only a local draft: the builder shows the rotation in force again, the save's and the cancel's refusal lines clear, the phone stepper returns to step 1, and focus moves to "Spremi rotaciju". From `sm` up, where the phone tab bar is gone, the bar is sticky 16 px above the viewport's bottom while there are unsaved changes and static otherwise; while it is sticky the page's scroll padding clears it, so a focused control never sits under it. On a phone it is never sticky: it stays in flow at the end, after Natrag / Dalje, so it never stacks on the tab bar, and the hint wraps above the buttons. CSS only.
- **Hour Band editor.** Bands are entered as a **name and a start time only**; the window, duration, and midnight-crossing flag are derived and shown read-only. A 24-hour bar renders the partition, with any gap hatched and flagged. Entering start times rather than ranges is what makes a gap or overlap unrepresentable instead of validated after the fact.
- **Pattern builder.** An ordered, reorderable list of shift-type steps of arbitrary length; the same shift type may appear more than once. Derived facts — cycle length, working steps, hours per cycle — update live beneath it.
- **Cycle preview.** Renders the next full cycle from pattern, offsets, and anchor date before saving, so the configuration is judged by its output rather than its inputs.
- **Hours table.** Tabular numerals; sortable, and filtered through the filter chips, where the team and the person combine. From 640 px it is a table that scrolls inside its own container; below 640 px each member is a stacked row (story 7.6): the name linking to their calendar month, `{team} · {n} smjena` under it, `Ukupno` over the total at the top right, then one cell per band and per leave column, each with its label, its hours and the band's shifts — a leave column as its days, `3 dana`, or `—` with none —, and the shifts in unresolved conflict only above zero, as `⚠` and words. The leave is one days column per absence type that draws down the allowance (story 13.2) — annual leave first, then the rest by name in Croatian collation, each labelled with the type's stored name — and one *Ostala odsutnost* column for every type that does not draw, only when the organization has one; a member's own *Sati* lists the same leave rows. Each leave column sorts by its days, and the shifts in unresolved conflict by their count. The table ends with a footer total (story 7.14): each figure column summed over the rows the filters leave, each leave column's days, labelled *Ukupno za prikazane osobe*; on a phone it is one line under the list, the total alone. The sort is one `Poredano: {column} ↑|↓` control above the rows, and an empty result shows its sentences and two ways out in place of the list.
- **Hours explanation (story 7.14, FR-42b).** Every hours figure above 0 on *Sati* — a member's total, each band and the leave; an admin's row figures in the table and on the phone — has an ⓘ, a 44 px ghost icon button named "Što čini iznos: {figure}" (with the person's name in the table; a leave column's ⓘ is named with that column's name). It opens the shared Dialog, a modal dialog (not a side sheet) of the shifts that compose the figure as an equation with dates: one line per shift in date order — the date as `23.09.2026`, then "{smjena} · {tip smjene} · {izvor}" with the source in words, *rotacija*, *promjena* (a shift-type override) or *zamjena* (a roster replacement), and the hours it adds with a `+` — then, under a rule, `=` and the figure. The leave is counted in days only (human decision 2026-10-10; since story 13.5 a half day's date adds `0,5 dana`, and its shift's worked half counts in the bands and the total by intersection, while the absent half is reported as leave — in days, never as hours, and in no band and not in the total), one leave column at a time (story 13.2), so its dialog, titled with the column's name, lists one line per charged leave date of that column — the date, "{smjena} · {tip smjene}" for each shift on it, and `+1 dan` — and `= 3 dana`; while the leave read is not ready it says it cannot be shown. A shift in unresolved conflict is marked on its line as on the view (FR-41): `⚠`, hidden from readers, and the words *čeka odluku*, never colour alone; a resolved conflict is not marked. The lines are the domain's operands and sum to the figure exactly. The total's dialog lists each shift with its whole duration; a shift across a band's edge is split only in the band dialogs, where it appears in each band it touches with that band's part. The table's footer totals have no ⓘ: the footer is the sum of the rows above it, each of which has its own. A figure of 0 has no ⓘ; a figure the domain cannot explain says so in a `Notice role="alert"`. The dialog's content is worked out once, as it opens, and a re-read never redraws it while open. Closing by the ✕, the backdrop or Escape returns focus to the ⓘ. It closes on its own when its ⓘ leaves the page — the figures give way to a message or the skeleton, or the organization's month crosses 640 px into the other layout — and does not reopen when they come back. A member's own page is titled *Moji sati*; an admin's remains *Sati*.
- **Hours export.** One secondary action on Organization hours, `Izvezi u Excel`, admin-only. It exports the current period, the rows the filter chips leave, and the sort, and nothing else. Every hours cell is a spreadsheet duration whose number format reads as the screen does — `192 h`, `12 h 30 min`, `30 min`, chosen by the screen's own rule — so a column still sums natively; the leave is one column per leave column of the screen (story 13.2), its days a plain number under `{name} (dani)` — the type's stored name, or *Ostala odsutnost (dani)* — since a number format cannot inflect `dan/dana`, empty at 0. The file ends with the screen's footer row, *Ukupno za prikazane osobe* in the Member column, its totals written as the rows are — durations and counts — so the file equals the screen. While the file is being built the action shows progress and is disabled. A filter that leaves no row closes it. There is no format picker in MVP. After the file downloads a status line (`Notice role="status"`, story 7.14) names it and how many people it holds — "Preuzeta je datoteka Sati DVD Mladost listopad 2026.xlsx s 17 osoba." It belongs to the view it was built from: another month, filter or sort, or a new export, removes it, and it is never a toast.

## State Patterns

- **Empty.** States what is true. Conflicts empty: *"Ništa ne čeka odluku."* Member with no team: an explanation, never a blank schedule. A member inactive on a date (or all month) is told so — `Neaktivno` on the day, "Ovaj mjesec tvoj status je neaktivan." / "Osoba {ime} ovaj mjesec nije aktivna." for the month — never "no team", which would name the wrong reason. A filtered *Sati* table with no row says why, never "no results": "Luka Knežević nije u smjeni Smjena B u listopadu 2026.", then "Luka Knežević je u smjeni Smjena A." or "Luka Knežević nije ni u jednoj smjeni u listopadu 2026.". It offers `Ukloni filtar: Smjena B`, which keeps the person, and `Poništi filtre`. Team names are data and never declined. A month with nobody says "U ovom mjesecu nema nijedne osobe.". A count that is zero is still shown — `0 čeka odluku` — because hiding it is indistinguishable from not having loaded.
- **Loading.** Skeletons matching final layout for the calendar grid and tables; no spinners on primary surfaces. A month already visited and a month never visited must feel the same. A navigation whose guard is still deciding (a slow session or role read) shows the page skeleton after a second, with no text; a read that has not settled in about ten seconds counts as failed, which sends the visitor to sign in (session) or to the first destination (role).
- **A screen that failed.** A route that throws shows "Nešto nije uspjelo", "Stranicu trenutačno nije moguće prikazati. Pokušaj ponovno." and a *Pokušaj ponovno* button that reloads the route. It never shows the error's text or a code; the cause goes to the console.
- **Offline.** A surface read online opens offline and renders its WHOLE last snapshot, from the copy kept on the device, under one `Notice role="status"` with a no-signal icon below the heading: *Bez veze · stanje od 10.10.2026 14:32*, the time being when its OLDEST read is from, in the organization's zone (story 8.2: *Danas* and *Kalendar*; story 8.2b: *Sati*, *Godišnji*, *Ljudi* — the admin's table and the members' directory — and a team's roster; story 8.2c: *Za odluku* — *Odlučeno* too, once opened online — a conflict's resolution page and the member page, under one note for the whole page; story 8.2d: the settings surfaces — *Organizacija*, *Satni pojasi*, *Postavke rotacije* and *Ljudi/smjene*, each with its editor dialog — so every surface now reads offline). A cached figure is never shown beside a fresh one or beside a missing one: if any of the surface's reads was never read on this device, the surface shows its unavailable state and no note. Offline, no surface offers the `Pokušaj ponovno` read retry there — its re-read would wait for the connection — so only the message shows; the message stays as it is, and the retry returns with the connection (story 8.2d for the settings surfaces, 8.2e for every other). On every surface, a retry that had focus when the connection dropped hands it to its message (story 8.2e). Online, a kept copy is never drawn in place of a fresh read: after a reload the surface shows its skeleton until every read is fresh. After a reconnect it keeps the cached snapshot and its note — an open dialog stays open — until every read is fresh, then the note goes and the whole surface renders fresh at once; if any read has no data, the skeleton. Offline, a sign-in whose token has expired is not a sign-out: the surfaces above still open on the copy, and the sign-in form appears only if the connection returns and the sign-in really has ended. Offline, a screen whose content depends on the role — every admin-only screen, and *Ljudi*, where a member gets the directory — opens on the role kept on the device, and with none it sends the member to the first destination (*Danas*, as `/` does) at once rather than waiting. The navigation, the member's name and the organization's name around a surface follow the same rule each on its own and carry no note: after a reload none is drawn until it is read again, offline each is drawn from the copy, and a name never read leaves the navigation standing. Signing out deletes the copy, and nothing of one account is shown to the next. Offline, *Odjava* always signs out: offline, or when the request never reaches the server, the device signs out locally at once (a server that answers with an error online still says *Odjava trenutačno nije moguća*), deletes the copy and opens the sign-in form (story 8.3a). The one thing the device keeps is the last organization's slug (`shift.lastOrganization`), which only prefills the sign-in form and is not organization data. **Writes offline** (story 8.3a). Nothing pretends to save offline: every write control on the work surfaces — the Kalendar roster and override forms and their removals and erasure confirmations, the resolution's *Spremi*, the member page's basics, allowance, team, status (with its withdrawal), password reset and leave record, amend and removal, and *Dodaj osobu*, with the *Pokušaj ponovno* under *Ne mogu provjeriti…* that asks the same write again — and the sign-in and set-password submits stay focusable but `aria-disabled`, and a note beside them says *Za ovo je potrebna veza.* (*Za prijavu je potrebna veza.* on the sign-in). A press does nothing: nothing is sent or queued, and no erasure check starts, so *Ne mogu provjeriti…* never shows offline — a check the connection dropped under shows the note instead. Dialogs still open, local controls (pickers, toggles, the rotation save bar's *Odbaci promjene* of a local draft, the theme) still work, and what was typed is kept. Reconnecting lifts the gate and sends nothing by itself. Story 8.3b gates the settings surfaces the same way — *Organizacija*'s nine dialogs — the eight settings dialogs (name, logo, accent, leave year, carry-over, fire ranks, email notifications, minimum members) and the absence-type dialog, one for adding and editing (the logo's *Odaberi sliku* stays a local pick), *Satni pojasi*'s add, edit and removal, *Postavke rotacije*'s shift type add, rename, times, cancel of scheduled times and archive, *Spremi rotaciju* and the cancel of a scheduled change with their erasure confirmations and retries, the override review's *Potvrdi*, the amend's save and the discard's confirm — a row's *Promijeni* and *Odbaci* only open their dialogs and stay ungated — and *Ljudi/smjene*'s add, rename and archive — so every write control in the app is gated. Offline, *Spremi rotaciju* says so through the note, never through the save's unavailable refusal. A check or a write the connection drops under shows the note too, never its refusal: the dialog it was asked from stays open, and focus goes back to the control that was pressed.
- **Error.** States what failed and what to do. A refused save keeps the entered values. A read that could not be done says so with `Pokušaj ponovno` beside the notice, which reads it again (a read already in flight is left to land); a read the database refuses, which only signing in again fixes, offers none.
- **Saved decision.** A status line, not a toast (decision 21). After a conflict decision lands the screen returns to the queue, and a `Notice role="status"` above the count names what was saved: the shift, the date and the member, and the outcome — for a replacement, who replaces them ("…, zamjena: Dino Grgić."). It travels in router state only — never the URL, never storage — so it is gone on navigation (the *Za odluku* navigation entry included) and on a reload, and nothing times it out. It stands above the queue whatever state the queue is in, and takes focus so it is announced.
- **Refusal, blocking.** Two validations refuse a save outright, and only two: Hour Bands that leave a gap or an overlap, and a Rotation Offset outside the cycle. Both are refused because the resulting state is not merely unwise but unrepresentable — an hour belonging to no band has nowhere to be counted. The refusal names the specific gap in hours and keeps every entered value.
- **Warning, non-blocking.** Coverage warnings, rest-gap warnings, replacement-member clashes, a replacement a leave amend or removal would leave rostered (story 5.4e, named in the preview or the confirmation before the save), and the conflicts a leave record creates or clears (story 7.12, in the leave dialog's preview or the removal confirmation) appear at save time with the consequence in numbers, and never block. They do not persist as standing banners — the pilot's own rotation legitimately triggers a rest-gap warning every time it is saved, so a persistent treatment would train admins to ignore it. Short-staffed is the second named exception, after conflicts (UX-DR23): it is warned of at every save that can cause it (story 11.2), and since story 11.3 it also stands on an admin's calendar grid while it lasts — the cell's `UsersThreeBold` glyph and 3 px `warning` bar, *Premalo ljudi* in its label and in the legend, and the day detail's line — derived on every read and never resolved, so it ends only when the roster, the absence or the setting changes. With the setting empty there is nothing to show.
- **Erasure, confirmed.** The one thing a configuration save waits on (AD-5, story 5.5a). When "Spremi rotaciju" would remove the cause of an unresolved conflict — the member no longer works a date they are on leave — a confirmation dialog opens before anything is written: "Promjena uklanja {count} stavki koje čekaju odluku", then one row per conflict by date and team ("{smjena} · {dan dd.mm.} · {tip}", "{ime} (odsutnost) · nova rotacija: {smjena} taj dan slobodna", or "… taj dan bez {ime}" when the team still works). Each row has two `aria-pressed` toggles, "Potvrdi brisanje" and "Zadrži", with nothing preselected and focus starting on the first row's confirm. "Spremi rotaciju" stays `aria-disabled` until every row is confirmed; a kept row says "Zadržana stavka: promijeni rotaciju ili se vrati na uređivanje.", and "Natrag na uređivanje" returns to the draft untouched. The draft's warnings show in the same dialog and never block. The list is derived again before the write; when it changed, it shows again undecided under "Popis stavki koje čekaju odluku se promijenio. Odluči ponovno za svaku." The save's notice then counts what went ("Uklonjene 2 stavke koje su čekale odluku."). Nothing is recorded: the conflict goes because its cause does. A save whose erasures cannot be checked saves nothing and says "Ne mogu provjeriti što bi promjena uklonila s popisa Za odluku." with "Pokušaj ponovno". The same dialog guards the calendar's roster changes (story 5.5b): saving a change in the roster dialog ("Promijeni sastav", story 7.9), and removing one from the day detail. It opens as its own dialog beside the day detail, in the calendar's words — the second line always reads "{ime} (odsutnost) · nakon promjene: {smjena} taj dan bez {ime}" (a roster change never makes the team free), a kept row says "Zadržana stavka: vrati se na uređivanje.", and its own save is "Spremi promjenu". For a removal the lede ends "Prije uklanjanja odluči za svaki.", a kept row says "Zadržana stavka: odustani od uklanjanja ili se vrati na dan.", and the save is "Ukloni". A change that erases nothing still saves in one click. A removal goes through its own neutral confirmation first; when its "Ukloni" finds erasures, that confirmation closes and the erasure dialog follows. "Natrag na uređivanje" returns to the roster form with its inputs kept, or to the day detail for a removal, and focus goes back to the control that opened it. A change whose erasures cannot be checked writes nothing and says so in the form, or in the removal's confirmation, with "Pokušaj ponovno". The member page guards its team and status cards the same way (story 5.5e): a move to another team or to no team, withdrawing a scheduled move, a deactivation, and withdrawing a scheduled reactivation. Since story 7.11 a move or a deactivation is checked by its dialog's one final button, and the erasure dialog opens over that dialog, which keeps what was entered; a withdrawal's own confirmation comes first, and when its confirm finds erasures it closes and the erasure dialog follows. Either way it is in the member page's words — the second line reads "{ime} (odsutnost) · nakon promjene: {smjena} taj dan bez {ime}", naming the team the conflict was on (a move to another working team still erases the old team's), a kept row says "Zadržana stavka: odustani od promjene ili se vrati na uređivanje.", and its save is the confirmation's own ("Potvrdi deaktivaciju osobe {ime}"). Its rows scroll inside the dialog, since a deactivation can erase many, with no page or sideways scroll at 390 px. "Natrag na uređivanje" returns to the team or status dialog with the team, position and date kept, and focus on its final button (on a withdrawal, to the card). A reactivation, withdrawing a scheduled deactivation and a position-only change only add and run no check; a change that erases nothing writes as before. A change whose erasures cannot be checked writes nothing and says so inside its dialog (or the withdrawal's confirmation), with "Pokušaj ponovno"; the saved line on the card counts what went. The calendar's shift-type overrides are guarded the same way (story 5.5f): setting a type in the type dialog ("Promijeni tip smjene", story 7.9), and removing an override from the day detail. The dialog opens beside the day detail in the calendar's words — the second line always reads "{ime} (odsutnost) · nakon promjene: {smjena} taj dan slobodna" (an override never changes who is rostered), a kept row says "Zadržana stavka: promijeni izmjenu ili se vrati na uređivanje.", and its own save is "Spremi izmjenu"; for a removal the lede ends "Prije uklanjanja odluči za svaki.", a kept row says "Zadržana stavka: odustani od uklanjanja ili se vrati na dan.", and the save is "Ukloni". The form's own checks (the projected type, the reason) come first. An override that erases nothing — from one working type to another, or making a free day working — saves in one click. A removal goes through its own confirmation first, then the erasure dialog. Removing an override pending review opens no erasure dialog, since it is not applied. "Natrag na uređivanje" keeps the type and reason as entered and returns focus to the control that opened it. An override whose erasures cannot be checked writes nothing and says so in the form, or in the removal's confirmation, with "Pokušaj ponovno"; the saved line counts what went. Cancelling a scheduled rotation change is guarded the same way (story 5.5g): the cancel reverts its dates to the previous version and brings back the overrides it had made pending, so the cancel's own confirmation runs the check first. When its "Potvrdi poništavanje promjene" finds erasures, that confirmation closes and a separate erasure dialog follows, in the cancel's words — the title "Poništavanje uklanja {count} stavki koje čekaju odluku", the lede ending "Prije poništavanja odluči za svaki.", the second line "{ime} (odsutnost) · nakon poništavanja: {smjena} taj dan slobodna" (or "… taj dan bez {ime}" when the team still works), a kept row saying "Zadržana stavka: odustani od poništavanja.", and its own save "Potvrdi poništavanje promjene". "Natrag" keeps the scheduled change and returns focus to the cancel's offer, or to "Spremi rotaciju" once the offer is gone; the dialog stays open, busy, while the cancel is sent. A cancel that erases nothing cancels after its confirmation as before; one whose erasures cannot be checked deletes nothing and says so inside the confirmation ("Ne mogu provjeriti što bi poništavanje uklonilo s popisa Za odluku."), with "Pokušaj ponovno"; a change no longer scheduled, or a dialog saved after the organization's day has turned, is the cancel's own stale refusal. The cancelled line counts what went ("Uklonjena 1 stavka koja je čekala odluku."), and the calendar and the queue show the revert without a reload. The rotation builder's override review is guarded the same way (story 5.5h): confirming or amending an override in "Izmjene za pregled" puts it in force, so both run the check first, in the review's words — the title "Izmjena uklanja {count} stavki koje čekaju odluku", the lede "Kad izmjena vrijedi, uklanja uzrok {count} stavke koja čeka odluku. Prije potvrde odluči za svaku." for a confirm or "Promijenjena izmjena uklanja uzrok … Prije izmjene odluči za svaki." for an amend, the second line always "{ime} (odsutnost) · nakon promjene: {smjena} taj dan slobodna", a kept row saying "Zadržana stavka: promijeni ili odbaci izmjenu, ili se vrati na uređivanje.", and its own save "Potvrdi izmjenu" (kept apart from the rows' "Potvrdi brisanje") or "Spremi izmjenu"; its rows scroll inside the dialog, which stays open, busy, until the write settles. A confirm that erases nothing stays one click; one that erases opens the dialog, and "Natrag na uređivanje" returns focus to that row's "Potvrdi" (or the review's heading). The amend's own checks (the projected type, the reason) come first, inside its dialog; when its save finds erasures, the amend closes and the erasure dialog follows, and "Natrag na uređivanje" reopens the amend with the type and reason as entered. An amend is never reopened over an override that has left the review: it is said as already reviewed or removed there instead. Since 0039 the review lists the roster changes a rotation change left pending too, in the same list by date and team: a row says the change in the day detail's terms ("Promjena sastava: {skida se} → {dolazi}", "… dolazi {ime}", "… skida se {ime}") beside the type the rotation now projects, its reason and author. Its "Potvrdi" and "Promijeni" are offered where a rotation version governs the date; "Promijeni" changes who arrives — "Promijeni tko dolazi: {smjena} · {datum}", with "Dolazi" grouped as the roster dialog's candidates (the member taken off and the one already put on are never offered) — and keeps who is taken off. Its erasure rows read "{ime} (odsutnost) · nakon promjene: {smjena} taj dan bez {ime}", since a roster change never makes the team free, and "Odbaci" asks "Odbaciti promjenu sastava za {smjena} · {datum}? Sastav se vraća prema rotaciji." Until it is confirmed or amended the calendar's day detail keeps it under "Promjena sastava čeka pregled" and applies the default roster. A confirm whose erasures cannot be checked writes nothing and says so in the review, an amend inside its own dialog, each with "Ne mogu provjeriti što bi izmjena uklonila s popisa Za odluku." and "Pokušaj ponovno"; the done line counts what went ("Uklonjena 1 stavka koja je čekala odluku."). Discarding a pending override asks nothing, since it is not applied. Since Epic 11 retro A7 the cancel and the review carry story 11.2's short-staffed block too, from the same "after" as their erasure checks, over the cached calendar: the cancel's confirmation above its buttons when reverting would newly leave a working shift under *Najmanje ljudi u smjeni* (over a year from the scheduled date), each review row under its buttons only when that row's "Potvrdi" would (for its date), and the amend's dialog for the type or member chosen now, recomputed on each change; the cancel and the amend say the neutral unknown line when it cannot be checked, a row says nothing then, and a discard never shows one. Each sits in its own polite live region, says nothing with the setting empty, and never gates a button.
- **Conflict.** Persisted product state, not a UI state. Visible on an admin's calendar without opening a detail view, counted on the admin dashboard, and queued soonest-first. It never expires, never auto-clears, and is never hidden from an admin. A member-role account sees no conflict mark and no uncovered mark anywhere on their calendar, because the decision is not theirs (story 7.10). In *Moj raspored* their own leave dates are marked with `◷`, named *Odsutnost* whatever the type (story 13.2b), including dates where they had no working shift. *Sve smjene* shows them no conflict and no one's leave, their own included, and their day detail states no conflict, so their legend never lists `⚠` or `◌`. This is the calendar only: *Sati* still counts a member's own shifts in unresolved conflict (story 5.3d), because those are their hours.
- **Overridden.** Identifiable on the calendar and in a schedule list without opening detail. Detail names author, timestamp, reason, and the projected value the override replaced.
- **Resolved conflicts (story 7.16).** *Za odluku* has two tabs, *Čeka odluku* — the default, the queue above — and *Odlučeno* (named *Neriješeni* and *Riješeni* until story 9.1). *Čeka odluku* carries its count on the tab (`Čeka odluku · 0` at zero too, never hidden; the section's own count line stays). *Odlučeno* lists each decided conflict with its date, member and team, the shift type the decision was made on (stored with it since 0038; an older decision names the type the schedule has today), the decision ("Prihvaćeno kao nepokriveno", "Zamjena: {ime}", "Izmijenjena odsutnost"), and who decided it and when ("Odluka: {admin}, {dd.mm.gggg} u {hh:mm}", in the organization's zone), newest decision first, with `0 odlučenih stavki` and "Još ništa nije odlučeno." when none. A decider who is no longer a member reads "administrator kojeg više nema u organizaciji". It is a read of stored decisions ONLY (FR-48a, Q11) — `conflict_resolutions` and, since story 9.5, `leave_request_decisions`: no entry is a link, and no other change history is shown, because it is not an audit-log screen (PRD §7.2). A resolution ends with its leave (0031), so an entry leaves the tab when the leave that caused it is removed or moved off its date, a replacement whose roster change no longer applies is back in *Čeka odluku*, and so is a decision whose day now has another shift type than the one it was made on (a decision never re-applies to a different shift; deciding again replaces it); a conflict is in exactly one tab. The tabs are a tablist with one tab stop (arrow keys, Home and End). **Requests beside collisions (story 9.5).** *Čeka odluku* lists each pending leave request beside the collisions, under the one count the tab, the heading, admin Danas and the resolution screen's back link all show (that screen's `K od N` and ‹ › stay over the collisions). A request is dated by its first day and sorted as a collision is — upcoming soonest first, then past most recent first, a request starting before today drawn as past — and on one date collisions come first, then requests by when they were sent, oldest first. Every row names its kind in words: a collision *Odsutnost u smjeni*, a request *Zahtjev za odsutnost*, with the member (or `Osoba više nije na popisu`) and `Odsutnost {od}–{do}` — since story 13.5b a half-day request's followed by its half, `Odsutnost 10.09.2026–10.09.2026 (prvi dio smjene)` or `(drugi dio smjene)` (admin Danas and *Odlučeno* do not name the half); the whole row opens its decision screen, and no cost or balance is shown on it. *Odlučeno* lists approved and rejected requests beside the decided collisions, newest decision first, each opening as a resolved conflict does — the request's first day, its badge (*Odobren* or *Odbijen*) and the kind — then the member, the range and `Odluka: {admin}, {datum} u {vrijeme}`; an approved request whose leave record an admin later amended or removed keeps *Odobren* and adds `Odsutnost je naknadno izmijenjena.` or `Odsutnost je naknadno uklonjena.` (0047, derived from the record, never a new status); a withdrawn request was never decided and is listed nowhere. Still read-only and still a read of stored decisions only. **Swaps beside them (story 12.4).** An accepted swap — one a colleague accepted, waiting for an admin — is the third kind, *Zamjena smjene*, under the same count: dated by the earlier of its dates (past when either date is), after collisions and requests on one date and by when it was sent; its row names `{predlagatelj} → {kolega}` and the given shift (`13.09.2026 · Smjena B`, with `· zauzvrat` and the shift taken in return) and opens its decision screen. A decided swap returns with *Zamjena odobrena: {predlagatelj} → {kolega}, {datum}.* or *Zamjena nije odobrena: …*, and *Odlučeno* lists it — approved, rejected or closed by a deactivation (*Odobrena*, *Nije odobrena*, *Zatvorena deaktivacijom*) — with the members, the shifts and `Odluka: {admin}, {datum} u {vrijeme}`; an approved one whose override was later amended or removed in the calendar keeps *Odobrena* and adds *Zamjena je naknadno izmijenjena u kalendaru.*, derived on read. A withdrawn or declined swap is listed nowhere. If the requests or the swaps cannot be read, the whole queue and the admin's *Treba tebe* are unavailable with their retry, never a partial list; offline they read the requests and the swaps from the same snapshot as the rest. The resolution screen does not stand on the requests or the swaps: if either cannot be read (failed, offline and not on the device, or not yet in), the screen stands and its back link names no number (retro 8–9 item 18).
- **Past-but-unresolved.** A conflict on a date already gone stays in the queue and is visually distinguished from upcoming ones. It is history that still needs a decision, not a task to hide.

## Interaction Primitives

- **Tap targets** 44 px minimum on touch, including calendar cells — which sets the real floor for grid density.
- **One destructive confirmation step**, never a colour-only signal, since `{colors.destructive}` is reserved for conflicts.
- **Confirmations are modal** (design refresh C). Removing, archiving, deactivating or reissuing a credential asks one question naming the subject, in a dialog, with the cancel and then the confirm at its bottom right. A deactivation's question also states its consequence in numbers (story 7.13c): while *Vrijedi od* holds a date, one neutral line under it says `{team} od tada ima {n} od {m} članova, a {u mjesecu} je to {k} smjena.` (`Smjena B od tada ima 3 od 4 člana, a u listopadu je to 7 smjena.`): the team's roster on that day, before and after the change, and the member's own duties on it from the day through the end of that month — touching shifts (a Noć and the Dan after it) are one duty, a shift alone is one, leave days are not excluded — the month clause dropped when none is left. It is computed from the calendar snapshot with the erasure guard's own "after", sits in the question the dialog is described by, and never gates the save; on no team, or while the calendar is not read, the question stands alone. Inside an edit dialog the question replaces the form in the same dialog, and a cancel returns to the form with what was typed. While the write is in flight, the dialog cannot be dismissed.
- **Add and edit in a dialog where the record is small** (design refresh C). An hour band, a shift type and a team are added in a dialog opened from their list, and edited in a dialog over that list. The route still names the record, so a link opens it and Back closes it. The dialog's close is the way back, so no "back" button sits beside it. A remove or archive offer sits beside Save, at the right, in neutral styling with its icon, as a short word whose accessible name names the subject. A member, with its password, status and team history, stays a page, and its way back sits above its title; since story 7.11 that page reads as facts and each of its changes opens its own small dialog (see *Member page*). Day detail (story 7.9) is facts, and each of its changes — the roster, the shift type — opens its own small dialog with one Save and a computed *Što se mijenja*. Organizacija (story 7.18) is a page of facts too, and each of its changes opens its own small dialog with one Spremi (see *Organization settings*).
- **A computed value is shown, not entered** (design refresh C). An hour band's end is the next band's start (AD-3), so the dialog shows it beside the start as the start is typed, never as a field.
- **A scheduled change stands out.** A team or status change dated after today is set apart on the member's page, and the member list marks a team change in the team cell ("Od {date}: {team}") and a status change in the status column ("Od {dd.mm.}: neaktivno|aktivno", story 7.13), so it is seen before it takes effect.
- **No bulk conflict resolution anywhere.** Each conflict is decided on its own screen with its own consequences shown. Amending a leave record still clears every conflict it caused — that is removing the cause, not batching decisions — but no affordance resolves a list of conflicts at once.
- **Optimistic updates are not used** for hours, leave balance, or conflict state. Those are the numbers the product is trusted for; a figure that flickers and corrects itself costs more trust than a brief wait.
- **Filter and mode state survives** month navigation within a session and is not persisted across sessions.
- **Month navigation** is symmetric in both directions and reaches every month the calendar represents (0001-01 to 9999-12; story 7.4). ‹ and › step one month, and so do PgUp and PgDn while focus is in the month control, never page-wide. The month picker reaches a far month in a few presses, so no month is unreachable and none is slower. Changing the month keeps the mode and the filters.

## Accessibility Floor

Behavioural. Visual contrast requirements are in `DESIGN.md`.

- **No information by colour alone**, anywhere. Every shift state carries a glyph or a fill treatment alongside its colour. This is verified in the compressed grid, in status pips, and in badges — the compact surfaces where the rule is most often broken.
- **Keyboard navigable**: calendar grid, both dashboards, the conflict queue, and the resolution screen. The resolution screen's options are a radio group, arrow-navigable.
- **Assistive technology** gets meaningful labels on every calendar cell: date, team, shift type, times, and any modifier — not a colour swatch and not a bare letter.
- **Both themes verified**, since a member may be in either without having chosen (the default follows the device).
- **No horizontal page scroll** at any width; wide content scrolls within its own container.
- **Glass never costs legibility** (story 15.5, UX-DR53). Text and boundaries on glass are measured against the worst thing that can sit under it, not the page alone: the blobs, and for chrome that content scrolls under and for a dialog every opaque fill, the ink button and the scrim. Text holds 4.5:1, boundaries, icons and the focus ring 3:1, in both themes.
- **Glass turns solid when it should.** A browser without `backdrop-filter`, and anyone who asked the OS for reduced transparency, gets opaque surfaces with a visible hairline. Under forced colours (Windows High Contrast) every glass surface is `Canvas` with a `CanvasText` border, the current destination keeps its underline, and a borderless glass button gains a `ButtonText` border so it still reads as a button.
- **Disabled and pending controls are exempt** from the contrast floor, as WCAG allows (inactive components); their measured values are recorded in DESIGN.md glass rule 4.
- **A low-end phone scrolls glass smoothly.** No frame spends over 50 ms in paint or composite while *Kalendar* scrolls, and the month still renders within two seconds (NFR-22); see DESIGN.md glass rule 7 for the measurement, a desktop proxy until a real low-end Android trace is taken.
- Target is WCAG 2.1 AA without formal audit in MVP. `[ASSUMPTION — carried from SPEC.]`

## Inspiration & Anti-patterns

**Register to reach for:** operational dashboards that a shift worker trusts without training. Quiet competence. A document rather than an application.

**Anti-patterns, named upstream and binding here:**

- **Dense enterprise grids with no hierarchy.** The failure mode this product must avoid most, because a rota genuinely is a dense grid — density is the requirement, illegibility is the failure.
- **A colour-coded spreadsheet ported to the web.** The thing being replaced. Reproducing it in HTML replaces nothing.
- **Consumer-app playfulness.** People plan childcare around this schedule.
- **Automatic helpfulness.** Anything that resolves, suggests, or defaults its way through a conflict defeats the product's central stance.

## Key Flows

Protagonist names and UJ ids mirror the PRD verbatim.

### Sign-in

Story 7.7. `/prijava` and `/prijava/<slug>` show one form: Organizacija, Korisničko ime and Lozinka, and one `Prijava` button. From the DVD's link the organization is the slug as a read-only row with `Promijeni`, and focus opens on the username. Without a slug, the field is prefilled with the last organization signed into on this device (`Zapamćeno na ovom uređaju.`, shown only while the field still holds it), or it is empty and focused, with the example `npr. dvd-kastel-novi`. Nothing is looked up before sign-in: no organization name or logo. A wrong organization, username or password gives one message, `Organizacija, korisničko ime ili lozinka nisu točni.`, every typed value stays, and focus moves to the password. The password has a show/hide toggle inside the field, and it is masked again on every submit. `Zaboravljena lozinka?` opens in place: the administrator sets a new password. The slug is remembered only after a successful sign-in.

First sign-in (story 7.8). A password an admin issues or resets is four lowercase words joined by hyphens, shown once to the admin in a monospace line with `Kopiraj` (`Kopirano.` or `Kopiranje nije uspjelo. Zapiši lozinku.`). A new member's password is shown in the add dialog's second step (story 7.13b), and a reset's in a dialog over the member page's *Prijava* card. A short or mismatched password is refused on the page with no request; the issued password itself is refused as `Nova lozinka mora biti drukčija od početne.`. After saving, the member continues without signing in again: to the link they opened, if one brought them here, otherwise to their landing destination. An admin reset puts them back under this step.
