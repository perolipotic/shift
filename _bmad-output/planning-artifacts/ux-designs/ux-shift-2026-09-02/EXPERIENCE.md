> **Peer contract.** This file owns *how it works*; `DESIGN.md` owns *how it looks*. Visual tokens are referenced here by name as `{colors.token}` and are defined there, never duplicated. Both spines win on conflict with any mock, wireframe, or import. Product requirements live in `SPEC.md` and its companions and are referenced, not restated.

## Foundation

**Form factor.** Responsive web application. Mobile, tablet, and desktop; no native application. Members are assumed to be primarily on phones — a volunteer between other tasks, in a vehicle, mid-shift, before bed. Admins are primarily on desktop or tablet, with every administrative task also completable on mobile, degraded in comfort but never in capability.

**UI system.** shadcn/ui on React + TypeScript + Vite + Tailwind. Both spines inherit from it. `DESIGN.md` specifies only the brand-layer visual delta; this file specifies only the behavioural delta. Where shadcn defines a behaviour — focus trapping in Dialog, dismissal in Sheet, filtering in Command — that behaviour is inherited and not respecified here.

**Themes.** Light and dark both ship. The default follows `prefers-color-scheme`; a three-option control (Sustav / Svijetla / Tamna) pins either theme per device (human decision 2026-09-25). It lives beside Odjava: in the *Više* sheet on a phone and in the profile menu on desktop (story 7.3). Consequence for every screen: state must be legible in both themes, and no state may rely on one theme's contrast.

**Localization.** Croatian is the only complete locale. Every string resolves through a translation key; none is hard-coded. See the spec's `localization.md` for the full contract — it is binding here, particularly the three-form plural rule, which affects every count this document specifies.

**Motion.** Minimal. Component transitions inherited from shadcn; the product adds none. No motion tokens exist.

## Information Architecture

*Rendered reference: [mockups/rotation-config-1.html](mockups/rotation-config-1.html) — Hour Bands and rotation configuration, desktop panel and phone stepper.*

**Navigation shape.** Bottom tabs on mobile, sidebar on desktop. Two layouts, one architecture.

**Phone bar (< 640 px) — four tabs and *Više*** (story 7.3). Five equal cells; the bar never scrolls sideways, so the current tab is always on screen. The four tabs are fixed per role: a member's are Danas, Kalendar, Sati, Godišnji; an admin's are Danas, Kalendar, Raspored, Ljudi. *Više* opens a modal bottom sheet (focus trapped, Escape and a backdrop press close it, focus returns to *Više*) that shows who is signed in (name, role · organization) and then, in binding order: *Pregled* — the role's other destinations every role reaches (Sati, Godišnji for an admin); *Postavke* — the admin-only ones (Postavke rotacije, Organizacija); *Prikaz* — the theme; and Odjava. A member's sheet holds only *Prikaz* and Odjava. While the page is a sheet destination, *Više* carries the active treatment. On desktop the sidebar lists every destination, and the theme and Odjava sit in the profile menu at its foot.

**Member Role — four destinations.** No configuration surface is reachable at all.

| Tab | Surface | Answers |

| Kalendar | Calendar | What does the month look like? |
| Sati | My hours | How much have I worked? |

**Admin — the four above plus configuration**, grouped in the sidebar:

- *Raspored* — Calendar, Conflicts (*Neriješeni*, and *Riješeni* to read what was decided, by whom and when)

The Leave Year's start is chosen as a day (1–28) and a month, never a date picker: it recurs yearly, and `0002` admits no day a February lacks.
- *Sati* — Organization hours

## Voice and Tone

Microcopy rules. Brand voice lives in `DESIGN.md` § Brand & Style.

- **State the fact, never the absence.** `Danas ne radiš` — never an empty area the member must interpret. Every empty state says what is true, not that nothing was found.
- **Numbers, not adjectives.** A warning states its consequence numerically: `24 h bez pauze`, `2 od 3 člana`. Never "significant", never "warning: check this".
- **No exclamation marks. No encouragement. No personality where a fact will do.** The member is not being congratulated for working nights.
- **Second person singular, informal** (`ne radiš`, not `ne radite`). A volunteer fire brigade is not a corporation. Consistent everywhere.
- **Times are ranges, dates are Croatian-formatted.** `19:00–07:00`, `12.09.2026`. En dash for ranges, never a hyphen.
- **Counts respect three plural forms** — one / few / other. `1 dan`, `2 dana`, `5 dana`; `1 konflikt`, `2 konflikta`, `5 konflikata`. A `count === 1` check is a defect.
- **Never say "shift" for a team.** `Smjena` is the team; `Tip smjene` is the shift type. Because `Sve smjene` reads ambiguously as an all-teams filter, that control shows a count — `Sve smjene (4)` — and groups its options under a labelled heading.
- **Rank and position** (sprint change 2026-09-25). The member's rank is `Vatrogasni čin`; the team role is `Položaj`, never *pozicija*; the setting is `Vatrogasni činovi i položaji`. The roster line is `Ime · čin · položaj`, labels lowercase beside the name. Neither is ever colour-coded, and neither is a filter in MVP.

## Component Patterns

*Rendered references: [mobile-calendar-1.html](mockups/mobile-calendar-1.html) (shift-cell, mode switch, duty-block) · [conflict-resolution-1.html](mockups/conflict-resolution-1.html) (resolution-option, consequence-strip, legend).*

Behavioural specs. Visual specs are in `DESIGN.md` § Components.

- **shift-cell.** Label always visible; time range appears when width allows and is dropped, never abbreviated, when it does not. Tapping opens Day detail. Modifiers compose — a cell may be both overridden and in conflict, and shows both glyphs.
- **Calendar mode switch.** A segmented control, two modes: *Moj raspored* (default on mobile for a Member Role) and *Sve smjene*. Mode persists across month navigation within a session.
- **Month toolbar (story 7.4).** *Kalendar* and *Sati* share one control, ‹ month ▾ ›, a group named "Mjesec". The month's name is a button that opens the month picker, a non-modal popover named "Odaberi mjesec". The picker shows the year with ‹ › for the year (disabled at the calendar's first and last year) and twelve months by short name, four to a row. The shown month is filled and marked current; today's month has an inset ring and "ovaj" under its name. Each month button is named in full, "Ožujak 2025" (", ovaj mjesec" on today's month), and the year is announced as it changes. The picker opens on the shown month's year, with focus on the shown month. ←/→ move by a month and ↑/↓ by a row, crossing into the year before or after; Enter or a press picks. Picking the shown month only closes it. Escape and a press outside close it and return focus to the month button. Tabbing out of it closes it and leaves focus where it went, and any change of the month (PgUp/PgDn, browser Back) closes it. Where ‹, › or the year's ‹ › reach a bound and disable under focus, focus moves to the month button, or within the picker to its month, never to the page. On the current month the month button carries "ovaj mjesec" as an outline Badge label inside it, and no `Ovaj mjesec` button is drawn; on any other month an `Ovaj mjesec` button follows the group, and pressing it moves focus to the month button. The month's heading stays for assistive technology, visually hidden: it names the grid and the day list and takes focus when a closed day detail's opener is gone. On a phone the label sits under the month's name so the toolbar fits 320 px, and the picker stays within the viewport.
- **Filter chips (story 7.5).** *Kalendar*'s *Sve smjene* and *Sati*'s organization table share one filter bar under the month toolbar, built from live records and never hard-coded options. Two chips, `Smjena: sve|<team>` and `Osoba: sve|<name>`, each a button that says it opens a dialog. An active chip carries its own 44 px ✕, named "Ukloni filtar smjena: Smjena B" or "Ukloni filtar osoba: Luka Knežević"; after it, focus goes to the next chip, or else to the first. From 640 px a chip opens its picker in the Popover; picking closes it and returns focus to the chip. The Smjena picker is one choice among `Sve smjene (4)` and each team, every team with its person count ("4 osobe"), the teams under a "Smjene" heading. The Osoba picker has a labelled search ("Traži osobu (17)") that narrows the list as it is typed, with diacritics ignored, and "1 od 17" beneath it. Below it are `Sve osobe (17)` and the people, grouped by their team for the month, with `Bez smjene` last. Its options are buttons in one tab stop that ↑ ↓ Home End move, the chosen one pressed. A person's team for the month is their membership on their last active date of the month, one rule for both screens. Under the chips a summary line (`role="status"`, always shown) says what is shown: "Prikazano: sve smjene (4) · 17 osoba", "Prikazano: Smjena B · 4 osobe od 17", "Prikazano: Luka Knežević · Smjena A", or on *Sati* "Prikazano: Smjena B i Luka Knežević · 0 osoba". While a filter is on, the line ends with one `Poništi filtre`, which clears both, keeps the screen and moves focus to the first chip. In *Kalendar* a person replaces the team: choosing one drops the team and hides the Smjena chip. On *Sati* the two combine. Below 640 px only active chips show, and `Filtri · N` (just `Filtri` with none on) or an active chip opens a bottom sheet on the Dialog; a picker left open closes when the viewport turns narrow. Focus after ✕ or `Poništi filtre` moves once the change has rendered, to `Filtri` where the chip it names is not drawn. It holds Smjena as radio rows with counts and Osoba as the same search list, with "U Kalendaru osoba zamjenjuje smjenu." in *Kalendar*. Its footer has `Poništi` and a live `Prikaži {n} osoba`. Every choice writes the URL at once, so Back works. The sheet closes on `Prikaži…`, Escape or a wide viewport, and every opening starts with an empty search and focus on the Smjena choice. All filter state lives in the URL (`?smjena=…&osoba=…`), kept across month changes. *Sati*'s old `?tim=` is replaced by `?smjena=` with every other parameter kept, while `sort=tim` stays a sort value. While the month loads, the toolbar shows and the bar waits for it.
- **Day detail (story 7.9).** One dialog of facts, with no form in it: the type and its times (or that the team does not work, or has no rotation), then for an admin each unresolved conflict of that team and date — "{ime}: godišnji {od}–{do}, a u sastavu je ove smjene." (without dates where the leave cannot be found, never dropped) under "Konflikt" or "Konflikti" with the calendar's own `⚠` and ring, each with "Riješi konflikt", a link to that conflict's decision screen — the roster as `Ime · čin · položaj`, and *Izmjene*: the shift-type override (or the one pending review) and the roster changes, each with its author, time and reason and, for an admin, its own "Ukloni", which opens the neutral confirmation; or "Za ovaj dan nema izmjena tipa smjene ni sastava.". An admin's footer has "Promijeni sastav" on a working day and "Promijeni tip smjene" where a type may be set; each opens its own small dialog beside the day detail, with one Save and a cancel, and closing it returns focus to its opener. The type dialog is the type `Select`, opening on "— odaberi tip smjene —" (a save with no type says "Odaberi tip smjene."), and the reason; the roster dialog is "Skida se", "Dolazi" and the reason, nobody preselected, with "Dolazi" grouped as native option groups by 5.4c's candidate groups (`slobodan`, `radi taj dan · 24 h bez pauze`, `na godišnjem taj dan`), which inform and never block; while the leave is not read, or the groups cannot be worked out, its members are listed ungrouped rather than guessed free. Each dialog's close is named after its change ("Zatvori promjenu sastava"). When a re-read means the day no longer offers the change, its dialog closes, the day detail says why and focus moves to the day detail. Each dialog computes *Što se mijenja* before saving, from the same "after" snapshot its erasure check uses: the type from and to ("Tip smjene: Noć 19:00–07:00 → Slobodno"), who leaves and who arrives, and each member whose month's hours move ("Sati: Ana, Dino, Eva i Ivo −12 h svakome"), nothing until a choice is made. A save that lands closes its dialog and the day detail says so in a `Notice role="status"`. A member's day detail is facts only: no conflict, no footer and no "Ukloni".
- **Member page (story 7.11).** `/ljudi/{id}` is headed by the person's name (`h1`), with the rank, the position and the team under it (`vatrogasni dočasnik · vozač · Smjena A`, each only where used) and the role and today's status as badges. It reads as fact cards, in this order: *Osnovni podaci* (username, address or "Nije upisana", role, rank), *Smjena* (the team today, a scheduled change as a callout), *Godišnji odmor {year}.* (*Pravo*, *Iskorišteno*, *Preostalo*, the records), *Status* (never on the caller's own row) and *Prijava* (the username the person signs in with). No field is mounted on the page outside the leave card's record form. Each card's header button opens its own small dialog with one final button: `Uredi` (name, username, address, role, rank; never the allowance), `Promijeni` (*Nova smjena*, *Položaj* after a pick, *Vrijedi od*), `Promijeni pravo` beside *Pravo* on the leave card (the allowance alone), and `Deaktiviraj` / `Ponovno aktiviraj` (*Vrijedi od* and the one neutral question naming the person and the date; its final button is the action itself, never `destructive`). Each button's accessible name names the person. *Nova smjena* opens on `Odaberi smjenu`, which cannot be saved ("Odaberi smjenu."); the current team is labelled `sadašnja` and is choosable only while positions are used, for a position-only change. The team and status dialogs' final button runs the preflight, then the erasure check, then the write, with no second confirm. A save sends only the fields its dialog shows. While its write is in flight a dialog cannot be dismissed; a refusal keeps it open with a `Notice role="alert"` above the buttons and what was entered; a landed save closes it, the card shows the new fact with a `Notice role="status"`, and focus returns to the button that opened it. A dialog whose change is no longer offered on a re-read closes by itself, and the card says the record moved. Withdrawing a scheduled team or status change stays a card button with its own confirmation, and `Dodijeli novu lozinku` keeps its confirmation, then shows the password once. The team dialog's *Što se mijenja* (decision 16) waits for a projection of a member's move across teams.
- **duty-block.** Groups consecutive working shifts that have no non-working interval between them into one duty. Headline is the end time; the span and total hours are metadata; one leg per constituent shift, marked done or in progress. **Presentation only** — the data remains two Scheduled Shifts on two dates.
- **resolution-option.** Radio-selection card. Exactly one selected at a time. No option is primary-styled, none is labelled recommended, and the order is fixed so muscle memory is possible. Nothing is preselected: `Spremi odluku` stays disabled until a card is chosen, and the line beside it says why ("Odaberi jednu od tri odluke."), then what saving records ("Odluka se bilježi s tvojim imenom i vremenom."). Story 5.4b ships the first card, "Prihvati kao nepokriveno"; 5.4c and 5.4d add theirs at positions 2 and 3. The three cards are one arrow-key group.
- **Amend leave (story 5.4d).** "Izmijeni godišnji odmor" at position 3 states a computed range that would clear this conflict, never a recommendation: with the conflict date `d` inside a record `from`–`to`, a `d` before the end moves the start to `d + 1` ("Mirela radi 02.10., a godišnji počinje 03.10. Otvara izmjenu godišnjeg s tim datumom."), the record's last day moves the end to `d − 1` ("… a godišnji završava 08.10. …"), and a one-day record is removed ("… a ovaj godišnji se briše. Otvara brisanje godišnjeg."). Chosen, `Spremi odluku` is ready at once and the line says "Spremi otvara izmjenu godišnjeg s datumom 03.10." (or "Spremi otvara brisanje ovog godišnjeg."). Saving writes nothing — no resolution row — and opens the member page, where the *Godišnji* card opens once: amend mode for that record with the range filled in and focus in the od field, or the record's removal confirmation. A record already gone opens nothing. The router state carries the hand-off, never the URL. While the page was reached from a conflict, "Natrag na konflikte" sits beside the way back to the list. A cancelled amend leaves the conflict in the queue; a saved one clears it by derivation.
- **Replacement guard (story 5.4e).** On the member page's *Godišnji odmor* card, an amend preview or a removal confirmation that uncovers a date on which someone replaced the member names that replacement, who stays rostered: one line each, "Dino Grgić ostaje na smjeni Smjena C · 02.10. kao zamjena. Ukloni zamjenu u kalendaru ako više ne treba.", ordered by date, then team. Only the dates the change uncovers count (a removal: the whole record; an amend: the old range less the new), and only a replacement still applied on the roster; one whose override was removed or is inert says nothing. The lines are notes, never `destructive`: nothing is disabled, gated or confirmed by them. In the amend form they sit inside the preview's polite live region. After the write lands, its status line repeats the lines captured just before it. If the resolutions cannot be read, one line says "Ne mogu provjeriti zamjene za ove datume." and the save still goes. The member's own *Godišnji* is untouched.
- **Replacement candidates (story 5.4c).** Choosing "Zamijeni osobu" opens "Tko odrađuje smjenu" after the cards — a sibling, because each card is a button and the cards are one arrow-key group — as its own arrow-key radio group with nobody picked. The candidates are the members active that date, less everyone on that team's roster and anyone already put on it, in three groups, each by name: `slobodan`, `radi taj dan · 24 h bez pauze` (a working shift on any team that date), and `na godišnjem taj dan` (which wins over the other two). Each line reads `Ime · čin · položaj`, as the roster form's, and wraps rather than truncates. The groups only inform: every candidate is selectable, none is disabled or recommended. With the card chosen and nobody picked, `Spremi odluku` stays disabled and the line says "Odaberi tko odrađuje smjenu."; pressing it moves focus to the first candidate. With nobody to pick at all, the line says "Nitko ne može preuzeti ovu smjenu. Odaberi drugu odluku." and pressing it returns focus to the card. Moving to another card drops the pick, so coming back starts with nobody. A candidate put on the shift meanwhile is refused by name ("… je već na ovoj smjeni. Odaberi nekoga drugoga."), the list is read again without them, and focus moves to it. The reason is clipped to 0026's 200 characters. The override is written with the reason "Zamjena za {ime} (godišnji)"; there is no reason field.
- **A replacement that no longer applies (story 5.5d).** A replacement counts only while its roster change applies. Once that change is removed in the calendar, left pending by a later rotation save, or inert (the replacement inactive that day, or already on the roster), the shift is really uncovered: the conflict is back in the queue, its ring is back on the calendar, and *Sati* counts the absent member's shift as work and as a conflict again, for the admin and for the member alike. Removing the change in the calendar ends the decision too, attributed to whoever removed it, and asks nothing. While a pending or inert change still holds the decision, the resolution screen says "Zamjena se ne primjenjuje. Ukloni je u kalendaru pa odluči ponovno." as the hint beside `Spremi odluku`, which stays disabled; "Ukloni je u kalendaru" links to that month in *Sve smjene*, narrowed to the team, where the day's pending or inert change is removed. A save that still reaches a held decision is refused with the same line. Accepted as they are (human, 2026-10-05): a decision is about the member's absence from that team's shift that day, so it survives a change of the shift type; a decision left behind by a team move hides nothing.
- **Resolution screen navigation.** ‹ › outline icon buttons beside the heading move to the adjacent unresolved conflict in the queue's order, without saving (decision 20, the navigation half). Each is disabled at its end, its label names the target's date, and both show at every width. The heading's line says the place in the queue ("2 od 7"), and the way back above it carries the count.
- **consequence-strip.** Three terms, always the same three, always in this order: coverage, the absent member's hours, the leave balance. Present on every resolution option so outcomes are compared rather than guessed. Three columns at every width, a phone's included, across the card under a divider. As shipped for "Prihvati kao nepokriveno" (story 5.4b): "3 od 4 člana" with lucide `CircleDashed` and "Nepokriveno"; "12 h kao godišnji" (`—` for a shift with no times); "16 dana preostalo" over "bez promjene", the balance of the leave year that holds the conflict's date. For "Zamijeni osobu" (story 5.4c): the coverage one higher ("4 od 4 člana"), then "· {ime}" once someone is picked, never "Nepokriveno"; the hours and the balance as the first card's, because the absent member stays rostered on leave. For "Izmijeni godišnji odmor" (story 5.4d): "4 od 4 člana · {ime} radi"; "12 h rada" (`—` untimed); "17 dana preostalo" over "+1 dan" — the balance once the dropped days are given back, and what that gives back, in the conflict date's leave year.
- **Legend.** Persistent wherever modifier glyphs render. Not a tooltip, not behind an info icon — four glyphs is a vocabulary, and a vocabulary needs to be on screen.
- **Configuration stepper (mobile).** Rotation configuration on a phone is a four-step sequence — shift types, pattern, offsets, preview — with completed steps navigable backwards. On tablet and desktop the same content is one scrolling panel with no stepper. Same data, same validations, same order.
- **Rotation save bar.** As shipped (story 5.5c, mockup `setup-1.html` §2, decision 25): the builder's one "Spremi rotaciju" sits in a full-width bar at the end of the builder, after "Povijest rotacije", on the card background behind a top border — not in the page header. The bar is a region named "Spremanje rotacije", rendered only once the builder has a draft. While the admin holds unsaved changes (their own draft, even one edited back by hand) the bar shows the hint "Promjena vrijedi od {datum}. · na snazi je rotacija od {datum}." (only the first part while no version is in force; nothing while `Vrijedi od` is empty), in a polite live region, and an outline "Odbaci promjene", disabled while a save is in flight. Discarding asks nothing, because it drops only a local draft: the builder shows the rotation in force again, the save's and the cancel's refusal lines clear, the phone stepper returns to step 1, and focus moves to "Spremi rotaciju". From `sm` up, where the phone tab bar is gone, the bar is sticky at the viewport's bottom while there are unsaved changes and static otherwise; while it is sticky the page's scroll padding clears it, so a focused control never sits under it. On a phone it is never sticky: it stays in flow at the end, after Natrag / Dalje and the history, so it never stacks on the tab bar, and the hint wraps above the buttons. CSS only.
- **Hour Band editor.** Bands are entered as a **name and a start time only**; the window, duration, and midnight-crossing flag are derived and shown read-only. A 24-hour bar renders the partition, with any gap hatched and flagged. Entering start times rather than ranges is what makes a gap or overlap unrepresentable instead of validated after the fact.
- **Pattern builder.** An ordered, reorderable list of shift-type steps of arbitrary length; the same shift type may appear more than once. Derived facts — cycle length, working steps, hours per cycle — update live beneath it.
- **Cycle preview.** Renders the next full cycle from pattern, offsets, and anchor date before saving, so the configuration is judged by its output rather than its inputs.
- **Hours table.** Tabular numerals; sortable, and filtered through the filter chips, where the team and the person combine. From 640 px it is a table that scrolls inside its own container; below 640 px each member is a stacked row (story 7.6): the name linking to their calendar month, `{team} · {n} smjena` under it, `Ukupno` over the total at the top right, then one cell per band and the leave, each with its label, its hours and the band's shifts, and the shifts in unresolved conflict only above zero, as `⚠` and words. The sort is one `Poredano: {column} ↑|↓` control above the rows, and an empty result shows its sentences and two ways out in place of the list.
- **Hours export.** One secondary action on Organization hours, `Izvezi u Excel`, admin-only. It exports the current period, the rows the filter chips leave, and the sort, and nothing else. While the file is being built the action shows progress and is disabled. A filter that leaves no row closes it. There is no format picker in MVP.

## State Patterns

- **Empty.** States what is true. Conflicts empty: *"Nema konflikata između godišnjih odmora i rasporeda."* Member with no team: an explanation, never a blank schedule. A filtered *Sati* table with no row says why, never "no results": "Luka Knežević nije u smjeni Smjena B u listopadu 2026.", then "Luka Knežević je u smjeni Smjena A." or "Luka Knežević nije ni u jednoj smjeni u listopadu 2026.". It offers `Ukloni filtar: Smjena B`, which keeps the person, and `Poništi filtre`. Team names are data and never declined. A month with nobody says "U ovom mjesecu nema nijedne osobe.". A count that is zero is still shown — `0 neriješenih konflikata` — because hiding it is indistinguishable from not having loaded.
- **Loading.** Skeletons matching final layout for the calendar grid and tables; no spinners on primary surfaces. A month already visited and a month never visited must feel the same.
- **Error.** States what failed and what to do. A refused save keeps the entered values.
- **Saved decision.** A status line, not a toast (decision 21). After a conflict decision lands the screen returns to the queue, and a `Notice role="status"` above the count names what was saved: the shift, the date and the member, and the outcome — for a replacement, who replaces them ("…, zamjena: Dino Grgić."). It travels in router state only — never the URL, never storage — so it is gone on navigation (the Raspored tab included) and on a reload, and nothing times it out. It stands above the queue whatever state the queue is in, and takes focus so it is announced.
- **Refusal, blocking.** Two validations refuse a save outright, and only two: Hour Bands that leave a gap or an overlap, and a Rotation Offset outside the cycle. Both are refused because the resulting state is not merely unwise but unrepresentable — an hour belonging to no band has nowhere to be counted. The refusal names the specific gap in hours and keeps every entered value.
- **Warning, non-blocking.** Coverage warnings, rest-gap warnings, replacement-member clashes, and a replacement a leave amend or removal would leave rostered (story 5.4e, named in the preview or the confirmation before the save) appear at save time with the consequence in numbers, and never block. They do not persist as standing banners — the pilot's own rotation legitimately triggers a rest-gap warning every time it is saved, so a persistent treatment would train admins to ignore it.
- **Erasure, confirmed.** The one thing a configuration save waits on (AD-5, story 5.5a). When "Spremi rotaciju" would remove the cause of an unresolved conflict — the member no longer works a date they are on leave — a confirmation dialog opens before anything is written: "Promjena briše {count} konflikt(a)", then one row per conflict by date and team ("{smjena} · {dan dd.mm.} · {tip}", "{ime} na godišnjem · nova rotacija: {smjena} taj dan slobodna", or "… taj dan bez {ime}" when the team still works). Each row has two `aria-pressed` toggles, "Potvrdi brisanje" and "Zadrži", with nothing preselected and focus starting on the first row's confirm. "Spremi rotaciju" stays `aria-disabled` until every row is confirmed; a kept row says "Zadržan konflikt: promijeni rotaciju ili se vrati na uređivanje.", and "Natrag na uređivanje" returns to the draft untouched. The draft's warnings show in the same dialog and never block. The list is derived again before the write; when it changed, it shows again undecided under "Popis konflikata se promijenio. Odluči ponovno za svaki." The save's notice then counts what went ("Uklonjena 2 konflikta."). Nothing is recorded: the conflict goes because its cause does. A save whose erasures cannot be checked saves nothing and says "Ne mogu provjeriti konflikte koje bi promjena izbrisala." with "Pokušaj ponovno". The same dialog guards the calendar's roster changes (story 5.5b): saving a change in the roster dialog ("Promijeni sastav", story 7.9), and removing one from the day detail. It opens as its own dialog beside the day detail, in the calendar's words — the second line always reads "{ime} na godišnjem · nakon promjene: {smjena} taj dan bez {ime}" (a roster change never makes the team free), a kept row says "Zadržan konflikt: vrati se na uređivanje.", and its own save is "Spremi promjenu". For a removal the lede ends "Prije uklanjanja odluči za svaki.", a kept row says "Zadržan konflikt: odustani od uklanjanja ili se vrati na dan.", and the save is "Ukloni". A change that erases nothing still saves in one click. A removal goes through its own neutral confirmation first; when its "Ukloni" finds erasures, that confirmation closes and the erasure dialog follows. "Natrag na uređivanje" returns to the roster form with its inputs kept, or to the day detail for a removal, and focus goes back to the control that opened it. A change whose erasures cannot be checked writes nothing and says so in the form, or in the removal's confirmation, with "Pokušaj ponovno". The member page guards its team and status cards the same way (story 5.5e): a move to another team or to no team, withdrawing a scheduled move, a deactivation, and withdrawing a scheduled reactivation. Since story 7.11 a move or a deactivation is checked by its dialog's one final button, and the erasure dialog opens over that dialog, which keeps what was entered; a withdrawal's own confirmation comes first, and when its confirm finds erasures it closes and the erasure dialog follows. Either way it is in the member page's words — the second line reads "{ime} na godišnjem · nakon promjene: {smjena} taj dan bez {ime}", naming the team the conflict was on (a move to another working team still erases the old team's), a kept row says "Zadržan konflikt: odustani od promjene ili se vrati na uređivanje.", and its save is the confirmation's own ("Potvrdi deaktivaciju osobe {ime}"). Its rows scroll inside the dialog, since a deactivation can erase many, with no page or sideways scroll at 390 px. "Natrag na uređivanje" returns to the team or status dialog with the team, position and date kept, and focus on its final button (on a withdrawal, to the card). A reactivation, withdrawing a scheduled deactivation and a position-only change only add and run no check; a change that erases nothing writes as before. A change whose erasures cannot be checked writes nothing and says so inside its dialog (or the withdrawal's confirmation), with "Pokušaj ponovno"; the saved line on the card counts what went. The calendar's shift-type overrides are guarded the same way (story 5.5f): setting a type in the type dialog ("Promijeni tip smjene", story 7.9), and removing an override from the day detail. The dialog opens beside the day detail in the calendar's words — the second line always reads "{ime} na godišnjem · nakon promjene: {smjena} taj dan slobodna" (an override never changes who is rostered), a kept row says "Zadržan konflikt: promijeni izmjenu ili se vrati na uređivanje.", and its own save is "Spremi izmjenu"; for a removal the lede ends "Prije uklanjanja odluči za svaki.", a kept row says "Zadržan konflikt: odustani od uklanjanja ili se vrati na dan.", and the save is "Ukloni". The form's own checks (the projected type, the reason) come first. An override that erases nothing — from one working type to another, or making a free day working — saves in one click. A removal goes through its own confirmation first, then the erasure dialog. Removing an override pending review opens no erasure dialog, since it is not applied. "Natrag na uređivanje" keeps the type and reason as entered and returns focus to the control that opened it. An override whose erasures cannot be checked writes nothing and says so in the form, or in the removal's confirmation, with "Pokušaj ponovno"; the saved line counts what went. Cancelling a scheduled rotation change is guarded the same way (story 5.5g): the cancel reverts its dates to the previous version and brings back the overrides it had made pending, so the cancel's own confirmation runs the check first. When its "Potvrdi poništavanje promjene" finds erasures, that confirmation closes and a separate erasure dialog follows, in the cancel's words — the title "Poništavanje briše {count} konflikt(a)", the lede ending "Prije poništavanja odluči za svaki.", the second line "{ime} na godišnjem · nakon poništavanja: {smjena} taj dan slobodna" (or "… taj dan bez {ime}" when the team still works), a kept row saying "Zadržan konflikt: odustani od poništavanja.", and its own save "Potvrdi poništavanje promjene". "Natrag" keeps the scheduled change and returns focus to the cancel's offer, or to "Spremi rotaciju" once the offer is gone; the dialog stays open, busy, while the cancel is sent. A cancel that erases nothing cancels after its confirmation as before; one whose erasures cannot be checked deletes nothing and says so inside the confirmation ("Ne mogu provjeriti konflikte koje bi poništavanje izbrisalo."), with "Pokušaj ponovno"; a change no longer scheduled, or a dialog saved after the organization's day has turned, is the cancel's own stale refusal. The cancelled line counts what went ("Uklonjen 1 konflikt."), and the calendar and the queue show the revert without a reload. The rotation builder's override review is guarded the same way (story 5.5h): confirming or amending an override in "Izmjene za pregled" puts it in force, so both run the check first, in the review's words — the title "Izmjena briše {count} konflikt(a)", the lede "Kad izmjena vrijedi, uklanja uzrok {count} neriješenog konflikta. Prije potvrde odluči za svaki." for a confirm or "Promijenjena izmjena uklanja uzrok … Prije izmjene odluči za svaki." for an amend, the second line always "{ime} na godišnjem · nakon promjene: {smjena} taj dan slobodna", a kept row saying "Zadržan konflikt: promijeni ili odbaci izmjenu, ili se vrati na uređivanje.", and its own save "Potvrdi izmjenu" (kept apart from the rows' "Potvrdi brisanje") or "Spremi izmjenu"; its rows scroll inside the dialog, which stays open, busy, until the write settles. A confirm that erases nothing stays one click; one that erases opens the dialog, and "Natrag na uređivanje" returns focus to that row's "Potvrdi" (or the review's heading). The amend's own checks (the projected type, the reason) come first, inside its dialog; when its save finds erasures, the amend closes and the erasure dialog follows, and "Natrag na uređivanje" reopens the amend with the type and reason as entered. An amend is never reopened over an override that has left the review: it is said as already reviewed or removed there instead. A confirm whose erasures cannot be checked writes nothing and says so in the review, an amend inside its own dialog, each with "Ne mogu provjeriti konflikte koje bi izmjena izbrisala." and "Pokušaj ponovno"; the done line counts what went ("Uklonjen 1 konflikt."). Discarding a pending override asks nothing, since it is not applied.
- **Conflict.** Persisted product state, not a UI state. Visible on an admin's calendar without opening a detail view, counted on the admin dashboard, and queued soonest-first. It never expires, never auto-clears, and is never hidden from an admin. A member-role account sees no conflict mark and no uncovered mark anywhere on their calendar, because the decision is not theirs (story 7.10). In *Moj raspored* their own leave dates are marked with `◷`, including dates where they had no working shift. *Sve smjene* shows them no conflict and no one's leave, their own included, and their day detail states no conflict, so their legend never lists `⚠` or `◌`. This is the calendar only: *Sati* still counts a member's own shifts in unresolved conflict (story 5.3d), because those are their hours.
- **Overridden.** Identifiable on the calendar and in a schedule list without opening detail. Detail names author, timestamp, reason, and the projected value the override replaced.
- **Resolved conflicts (story 7.16).** *Raspored* has two tabs, *Neriješeni* — the default, the queue above — and *Riješeni*. *Neriješeni* carries its count on the tab (`Neriješeni · 0` at zero too, never hidden; the section's own count line stays). *Riješeni* lists each decided conflict with its date, member and team, the shift type, the decision ("Prihvaćeno kao nepokriveno", "Zamjena: {ime}", "Izmijenjen godišnji odmor"), and who decided it and when ("Odluka: {admin}, {dd.mm.gggg} u {hh:mm}", in the organization's zone), newest decision first, with `0 riješenih konflikata` and "Još nijedan konflikt nije riješen." when none. A decider who is no longer a member reads "administrator kojeg više nema u organizaciji". It is a read of `conflict_resolutions` ONLY (FR-48a, Q11): the entry is not a link, and no other change history is shown, because it is not an audit-log screen (PRD §7.2). A resolution ends with its leave (0031), so an entry leaves the tab when the leave that caused it is removed or moved off its date, and a replacement whose roster change no longer applies is back in *Neriješeni*; a conflict is in exactly one tab. The tabs are a tablist with one tab stop (arrow keys, Home and End).
- **Past-but-unresolved.** A conflict on a date already gone stays in the queue and is visually distinguished from upcoming ones. It is history that still needs a decision, not a task to hide.

## Interaction Primitives

- **Tap targets** 44 px minimum on touch, including calendar cells — which sets the real floor for grid density.
- **One destructive confirmation step**, never a colour-only signal, since `{colors.destructive}` is reserved for conflicts.
- **Confirmations are modal** (design refresh C). Removing, archiving, deactivating or reissuing a credential asks one question naming the subject, in a dialog, with the cancel and then the confirm at its bottom right. Inside an edit dialog the question replaces the form in the same dialog, and a cancel returns to the form with what was typed. While the write is in flight, the dialog cannot be dismissed.
- **Add and edit in a dialog where the record is small** (design refresh C). An hour band, a shift type and a team are added in a dialog opened from their list, and edited in a dialog over that list. The route still names the record, so a link opens it and Back closes it. The dialog's close is the way back, so no "back" button sits beside it. A remove or archive offer sits beside Save, at the right, in neutral styling with its icon, as a short word whose accessible name names the subject. A member, with its password, status and team history, stays a page, and its way back sits above its title; since story 7.11 that page reads as facts and each of its changes opens its own small dialog (see *Member page*). Day detail (story 7.9) is facts, and each of its changes — the roster, the shift type — opens its own small dialog with one Save and a computed *Što se mijenja*.
- **A computed value is shown, not entered** (design refresh C). An hour band's end is the next band's start (AD-3), so the dialog shows it beside the start as the start is typed, never as a field.
- **A scheduled change stands out.** A team or status change dated after today is set apart on the member's page, and the member list marks it in the team cell ("Od {date}: {team}"), so it is seen before it takes effect.
- **No bulk conflict resolution anywhere.** Each conflict is decided on its own screen with its own consequences shown. Amending a leave record still clears every conflict it caused — that is removing the cause, not batching decisions — but no affordance resolves a list of conflicts at once.
- **Optimistic updates are not used** for hours, leave balance, or conflict state. Those are the numbers the product is trusted for; a figure that flickers and corrects itself costs more trust than a brief wait.
- **Filter and mode state survives** month navigation within a session and is not persisted across sessions.
- **Month navigation** is symmetric in both directions and reaches every month the calendar represents (0001-01 to 9999-12; story 7.4). ‹ and › step one month, and so do PgUp and PgDn while focus is in the month toolbar, never page-wide. The month picker reaches a far month in a few presses, so no month is unreachable and none is slower. Changing the month keeps the mode and the filters.

## Accessibility Floor

Behavioural. Visual contrast requirements are in `DESIGN.md`.

- **No information by colour alone**, anywhere. Every shift state carries a glyph or a fill treatment alongside its colour. This is verified in the compressed grid, in status pips, and in badges — the compact surfaces where the rule is most often broken.
- **Keyboard navigable**: calendar grid, both dashboards, the conflict queue, and the resolution screen. The resolution screen's options are a radio group, arrow-navigable.
- **Assistive technology** gets meaningful labels on every calendar cell: date, team, shift type, times, and any modifier — not a colour swatch and not a bare letter.
- **Both themes verified**, since a member may be in either without having chosen (the default follows the device).
- **No horizontal page scroll** at any width; wide content scrolls within its own container.
- Target is WCAG 2.1 AA without formal audit in MVP. `[ASSUMPTION — carried from SPEC.]`

## Inspiration & Anti-patterns

**Register to reach for:** operational dashboards that a shift worker trusts without training. Quiet competence. A document rather than an application.

**Anti-patterns, named upstream and binding here:**

- **Dense enterprise grids with no hierarchy.** The failure mode this product must avoid most, because a rota genuinely is a dense grid — density is the requirement, illegibility is the failure.
- **A colour-coded spreadsheet ported to the web.** The thing being replaced. Reproducing it in HTML replaces nothing.
- **Consumer-app playfulness.** People plan childcare around this schedule.
- **Automatic helpfulness.** Anything that resolves, suggests, or defaults its way through a conflict defeats the product's central stance.

## Key Flows

Protagonist names and UJ ids mirror the PRD verbatim.

- **UJ-1 — Damir configures the rotation once and retires the spreadsheet.** Fresh Admin account, empty Organization. Sets identity and Hour Bands (timezone and locale came with provisioning); defines three Shift Types; builds a four-slot Rotation Pattern; creates four Teams and gives each an Offset against a shared Anchor Date. Save reports coverage — one team on each working type every day — and a rest-gap warning stating 24 continuous hours, which Damir reads and accepts. **Climax:** the calendar shows three months already correct, without a date having been typed. - **UJ-2 — Luka checks whether they are working, from the truck.** Authenticated from a previous session, opens on *Danas*. If mid-duty, a duty-block headlines the end time with a progress bar. If not working, the screen says `Danas ne radiš` and shows the next working shift with both times. Hours and leave sit below; the next seven days follow as a list. **Climax:** answered before anything was tapped.
- **UJ-3 — Damir records Ana's leave and finds the collisions before they bite.** Opens Ana's record — 30 allocated, 12 used, 18 remaining — enters 10.09–16.09, and sees the cost in Leave Days before saving, counting only dates Ana was rostered to work. - **UJ-4 — Damir closes the month.** Opens Organization hours, picks September, sees every Member with shift counts, hours per band, total, and leave hours separately. ### Sign-in

Story 7.7. `/prijava` and `/prijava/<slug>` show one form: Organizacija, Korisničko ime and Lozinka, and one `Prijava` button. From the DVD's link the organization is the slug as a read-only row with `Promijeni`, and focus opens on the username. Without a slug, the field is prefilled with the last organization signed into on this device (`Zapamćeno na ovom uređaju.`, shown only while the field still holds it), or it is empty and focused, with the example `npr. dvd-kastel-novi`. Nothing is looked up before sign-in: no organization name or logo. A wrong organization, username or password gives one message, `Organizacija, korisničko ime ili lozinka nisu točni.`, every typed value stays, and focus moves to the password. The password has a show/hide toggle inside the field, and it is masked again on every submit. `Zaboravljena lozinka?` opens in place: the administrator sets a new password. The slug is remembered only after a successful sign-in.

First sign-in (story 7.8). A password an admin issues or resets is four lowercase words joined by hyphens, shown once to the admin in a monospace line with `Kopiraj` (`Kopirano.` or `Kopiranje nije uspjelo. Zapiši lozinku.`). A member who signs in with it reaches only `/postavi-lozinku`, `Postavi svoju lozinku`, until they save their own: `Nova lozinka` with the show/hide toggle, `Ponovi novu lozinku`, two live checks (`Najmanje 10 znakova (upisano: {count})`, `Lozinke se podudaraju`), `Spremi i nastavi` and `Odjava`. A short or mismatched password is refused on the page with no request; the issued password itself is refused as `Nova lozinka mora biti drukčija od početne.`. After saving, the member continues to their landing destination without signing in again. An admin reset puts them back under this step.
