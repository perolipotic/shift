Konfiguracija rotacije i satnih pojasa

The two hardest admin surfaces — the ones where layout drives behaviour, and the ones UJ-5 depends on. Desktop panel plus the phone version, because the spine requires every admin task to complete on a phone and this is the case that proves it.

Two rules this screen has to make visible rather than merely enforce. Hour Bands are entered as start times only and their windows are derived, so a gap or an overlap is structurally unrepresentable rather than validated after the fact. And nominal duration is always computed from the times — never typed — so a 19:00→07:00 shift cannot be recorded as anything but 12 hours.
