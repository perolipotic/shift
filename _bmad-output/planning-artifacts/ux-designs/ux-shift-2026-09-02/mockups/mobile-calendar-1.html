Mobilni kalendar & 24-satna dužnost

Operativna palette, glyph + fill encoding, Geist. All three phones are 390 px wide — actual phone width, not a shrunk desktop. Content is the pilot's: Smjena A–D, Dan 07:00–19:00, Noć 19:00–07:00, Slobodno. Toggle your OS to dark and reload — both themes render here.

2 · The 24-hour duty problem

The pilot works Dan straight into Noć — one continuous 24-hour stretch, correctly modelled as two shifts on two dates. A member finishing at 07:00 must not be shown two unrelated shifts with a gap. Two treatments:

D · Duty block (merged presentation)

Consecutive working shifts with no gap present as one dužnost with an end time and progress. The data model stays two shifts; only the presentation merges.
