# Direction G, "Staklo" (glass) — Kalendar mockups

Chosen on 2026-10-30 from seven directions (A–G) for the Kalendar screen. The decision record is `../../sprint-change-proposal-2026-10-30-glass-redesign.md` (D1–D10 accepted as recommended).

- `kalendar-g-desktop.dc.html`: desktop. Glass sidebar, a week view, the mini month and a conflict card.
- `kalendar-g-mobitel.dc.html`: phone. A day view with a week strip and a floating glass tab bar.

These are artboards from a Design canvas (`.dc.html`). They need that canvas's runtime (`support.js`) to render, so they are a visual reference, not a page to open on its own. The Phosphor icons are inlined as SVG (MIT).

They are a reference for **look only**, in the same way as the `shiftapp-v2` mockups. Per D4, D6, D7 and D10 these are not adopted:

- the indigo slot-2 colour;
- the week and day views;
- the red count badge;
- the words "konflikt" and "Riješi";
- the sidebar order.
