- (question) For the leave-counting discussion, the point that decides it: under the pilot's 4-slot rotation a member works 2 dates in every 4, so a leave range covers working shifts about half the time. Counting day by day therefore charges the member for dates they were already off - a Monday-to-Friday range costs 5 even though they were only rostered on two of those days. That is precisely the factor-of-two the spec's assumption flagged, and it is the thing to put to the fire department in those words rather than as 'calendar or working days', which they may hear as a bookkeeping question rather than as 'is a 30-day allowance worth 30 days off or 60'.
