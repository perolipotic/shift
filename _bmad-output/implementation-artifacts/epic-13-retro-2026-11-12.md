### Cross-story boundaries (diff-scope review)

- **B1. A members cache restored from before 13.3 crashes the member page** (fix now)
  - The persisted `['members']` entry holds *mapped* rows. The snapshot buster is the user id only (`lib/offline/persistence.ts:377,444`), and a snapshot may live 30 days (`:60`).
  - A row mapped before 13.3 has no `leaveYearStart` or `leaveAllowance`. `memberAllowanceOn` tests `member.leaveYearStart === null` (`members/services/list.ts:715`), so `undefined` passes the test.
  - `leaveYearNumberOf(today, undefined)` then throws in `checkLeaveYearStart` (`packages/domain/src/leave.ts:322`). It is reached in render through `use-member-basics.ts:114`, so the error boundary shows.
  - Godišnji, the member card, the overview and the review catch the same throw and show "unavailable" until a refetch.
  - Lesson: 13.3 changed the shape of a persisted *mapped* entry without a buster or a tolerant reader. Every earlier column (type, half) went through raw-row parsers, which are tolerant (`leave-list.ts:129`, `leave-request-list.ts:269,295`).
- **B2. Days are carried in from a year in which the member was not active** (defer, PO decision)
  - `carriedInto` (`leave.ts:750-767`) ends the previous year at its allowance plus consumed minus drawn and never reads `input.statuses`.
  - So a member created or reactivated this year shows *Preneseno* of up to the cap, as if a full unused year had been carried.
  - The 13.4b rule was followed literally. No story owned the member-status side.
- **B3. The admin's amend refuses a stored half whose shift has gone, even a type-only amend** (fix now)
  - `leavePreviewStateOf` defaults `refuseHalf = true` (`leave-section.ts:736,749`), and the amend dialog opens on the record's own half (`use-member-leave.ts:259,339`).
  - 13.5's Design Note says such a record costs 0 and raises nothing. 13.5b made the request review the only `refuseHalf: false` caller.
- **B4. On the member's own *Danas*, a half day reads as a whole day of leave**
  - `today.ts:262,344-386` ignores `half` and joins the half to adjacent records. Admin *Danas* counts the member at work (`admin-today.ts:680`).
  - Already open in `deferred-work.md` (13.5 review). Folded into the half-day PO batch (A5).
- **B5. Notifications and the remaining decision surfaces name a half as a whole-day range**
  - An amend that changes only the half sends the same text as before it (`notify/messages.ts:31-33`, `0059:139-142`).
  - Already open in `deferred-work.md` (13.5 and 13.5b entries). Folded into A5.
- Checked and clean:
  - Half days through carry-over cap, expiry and the consumed-first order: `intersect` keeps the half (`leave.ts:344`), and `2,5` formats and pluralizes correctly.
  - Non-drawing types × half × approval: 0059 copies the type and the half.
  - Type-name visibility: RLS holds at `0028:130` and `0040:255`, and no notification names a type.
  - Raw-row caches tolerate a missing type and a missing half.
  - Migration replacements 0052–0059: no later body drops a lock, refusal, sweep, notify call or grant.
  - E2E cleanup order: `database-helper.ts:223,498,690,1220-1261`.
