## Action items

All of them are remediation proposals. The product owner decided on 2026-10-30 to fix them, with every open item from earlier retros, in **one batch story** through `bmad-build`.

- A1: Make a provider stop channel-scoped, so a Resend 401/403 never releases push rows (F1), with a mixed-batch test. Owner: Dev.
- A2: Re-check device ownership at send time, so an endpoint handed to another member in the lease never receives the earlier member's payload (F2). Owner: Dev.
- A3: Make the claim settle rows whose recipient is not active today as failed, on both channels (F3). Owner: Dev.
- A4: Give the decision the same recipient rules as every other event on both channels: not the actor, active today, and email only with an address (F4; PO decision, 2026-10-30). Owner: Dev.
- A5: A signed-in visitor on `/prijava/<slug>` of another organization is offered that organization, not silently redirected in the current one (F5). Owner: Dev.
- A6: Make the push link resolve on the worker's own origin, through a path-only url or a deploy check (F6). Owner: Dev.
- A7: Make `notify`'s time budget cover the device delete (F7). Owner: Dev.
- A8: DEPLOY.md: one order for the first deploy of 0042–0045, and a rollback warning (F10). Owner: Dev.
- A9: Push and *Više* copy that matches the switches (F11). Owner: Dev.
- A10: Amend the architecture spine: AD-9 (exceptions, the delete RPC, the register definer), AD-14 (claim returns, three RPCs), AD-17 (`VITE_VAPID_PUBLIC_KEY`), AD-18 as shipped (F13). Owner: Architect (in the batch).
- A11: Docs and spec hygiene: F15, F16, three stale deferred-work entries, and a deferred-work entry for the rls-isolation split. Owner: Dev.
- A12, process: fix the time-of-day flake in `today.spec.ts:529`, and make main red block merges (P1, retro 8–9 item 22). Owner: Dev and PO.
- A13, process: in a `bmad-build` customization, (a) a spec that amends an AD must carry a spine-edit task, (b) a "decide in X" deferral becomes a required input of X's spec, and (c) a story that resolves a deferral marks the entry (P2, P3, retro 8–9 item 23). Owner: Dev.
- A14, process: parallel sessions must not share one dev-server port silently. E2E refuses a server it did not start, or each worktree gets its own port (P4). Owner: Dev.
