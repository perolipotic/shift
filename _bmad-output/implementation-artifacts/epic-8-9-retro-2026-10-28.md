update/delete, column grant of three columns), `leave_request_decisions` (no session
writes, composite FKs, one decision per request), both definers (`search_path = ''`,

lock, `closed_at is null` predicate, one transaction, races tested in
`test/concurrent-writes.test.ts:1164-1321`), and device persistence (owner-tagged
snapshot, `forget()` on every sign-out path, allowlisted keys, mutations never kept).
