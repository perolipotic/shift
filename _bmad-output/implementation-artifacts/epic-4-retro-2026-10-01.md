### Code review (cross-story boundaries)

- **C1. A member's name on Sati leads to the unfiltered calendar when that member is no longer active today.** — *fix.*
  - `calendarLinkSearchOf` (`organization-hours.ts:147-149`) links every row, and a row exists for anyone active on any date of the month (`:152-182`).
  - But the calendar accepts `osoba` only for members active today (`calendar/utils/month.ts:924-925`, `chosenPersonOf` at `:882-886`).
  - So when an admin opens last month and clicks someone deactivated since, the whole-organization grid opens. The "explain any figure" path breaks for exactly the people who left.
  - The tests cover only members who are active today (`hours.spec.ts:407-411`, `organization-hours.test.ts:288`).
- **C2. A member put on another team's shift while working their own is counted twice, with no marker.** — *defer, decision needed.*
  - `hours.ts:174-199` sums every shift of `memberScheduleOfMonth`, and `schedule.ts:309-323` adds the shifts a roster override puts the member on.
  - The 3.6b form offers such candidates and says "nothing is warned, blocked or suggested" (`calendar/utils/day-detail.ts:616-624`).
  - Result: 24 h for one 12 h window, on Sati, in the table and in the file.
  - This is a 3.6 design choice that hours now makes visible in totals. It is not the leave conflict that FR-41 and Epic 5 describe.
- **C3. The export leaves out the untimed-shift note the table shows.** — *defer.* In the file, shifts × length ≠ Total, with no explanation (`hours-export.ts:98-105` against `organization-hours-table.tsx:145-147`).
- **C4. The untimed note gives an organization-wide sum, not the rows it applies to.** — *defer.* `row.untimedShiftCount` is computed but never drawn per row (`organization-hours.ts:370-374, 416`).
- **C5. A team or person filter that matches no row in the new month is silently dropped.** — *defer.* The URL keeps `tim=X`, but the next change erases it (`organization-hours.ts:392-393, 409-415`, `use-hours.ts:55`).
- **C6. A member who changes team mid-month is filed entirely under their last team.** — *accept, by design* (`organization-hours.ts:184`, spec-4-2 matrix). Summing per-team exports misattributes those hours. Recorded as a known property.
- **C7. The calendar now refuses to load if band or organization-name validation fails.** — *accept, latent* (`calendar/services/snapshot.ts`, `readCalendar`). Database constraints make this unreachable today. It is coupling, not a live bug.
- **C8. The admin table recomputes the schedule input per member.** — *defer, performance.* `organization-hours.ts:178-179` → `memberScheduleInputOf`. Override standings are WeakMap-memoized per snapshot (`month.ts:78, 116`), so the remaining cost is per-member shift-type checks. Fine at pilot scale.
