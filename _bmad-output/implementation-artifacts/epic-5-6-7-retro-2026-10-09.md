### Process lessons

- **L1. PRs are merged before CI finishes, and once with a red test job.**
  - PR #180 (7.12) merged at 19:40:55Z. "Test (Vitest, local Supabase)" failed at 19:44:46Z, and `0ea1b7b` (#181) repaired `main`.
  - E2E was still running at merge for #175, #176, #182, #183, #185, #186 and #187.
  - `main` has no branch protection (GitHub API: "Branch not protected").
  - *Lesson:* make Test and E2E required status checks.
- **L2. Three stories needed a fix-up commit after merge.**
  - 7.9 merged before its review. `d6499f3` brought 9 review fixes, including a new refusal code and a deviation recorded only after the merge.
  - 7.6's no-sideways-scroll AC failed only in a full e2e run, because another test leaves a long-name member behind (`de076e8`).
  - 7.12 is the L1 case.
  - *Lesson:* do not merge while a story is in review or CI is pending.
- **L3. The Epic 4 lesson (Epic AC Deviations plus a ledger entry for every deferral) mostly held.**
  - Every `spec-5-*` file has the section, and most deferrals are ledgered.
  - Gaps remain: R8, R9, R10, R11, and R6 for 5.5f/g/h.
  - "Done" was also flipped on a split parent (7.13) whose last part was still open.
  - *Lesson:* closing a story requires all of its sub-parts done, or the AC moved explicitly.
- **L4. Bookkeeping lags behind the code.** Epics 5 and 6 are still `in-progress`, spec statuses are stale, and context files still say "in review". Each story added a separate "chore: mark done" PR (about 20 of the 72 commits) and still missed some.
